# Lesson 36: The *-tud* Participle and the Impersonal

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 271 · § 334–358 · [← Previous](lesson-35.md) · [Next →](lesson-37.md)

## Key ideas

- The *-tud* participle means "done to" and works like an adjective that never changes.
- The impersonal voice describes an action without naming who does it.
- Every impersonal form can be built from the *-tud* participle.
- The total object of an impersonal verb is usually nominative.

## Forming the participle (§ 334–338)

The participle is the fourth basic verb form, after the *-ma* infinitive, the *-da* infinitive, and the present tense.

| Ending | Comes after | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **-tud** | short vowel, *s*, *h* | *õpitud, kirjutatud, makstud, tehtud* |
| **-dud** | long vowel, diphthong, *l*, *n*, *r* | *saadud, müüdud, käidud, kuuldud, pandud, naerdud* |

Two exceptions take *-tud* where you would expect *-dud*:

| Cause | Examples |
|---|---|
| *-d-* before *-ma* | *andma → antud, hoidma → hoitud* |
| Vowel change in the stem | *siduma → seotud, pidama → peetud, kudama → kootud* |

## Using it as an adjective (§ 339–341)

| Use | Example | English |
|---|---|---|
| Before a noun | *värvitud sein* | a painted wall |
| After *olema* | *Sein on värvitud.* | The wall is painted. |
| Set phrase | *Hästi tehtud!* | Well done! |

The participle does not change, even when its noun does:

| Case | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Nom. | *suletud uks* | *suletud uksed* |
| Gen. | *suletud ukse* | *suletud uste* |
| Part. | *suletud ust* | *suletud uksi* |

Dropping the final *-d* makes a noun that does decline: *vigastatu, vigastatu, vigastatut, vigastatuid* "injured person". *Palju vigastatuid* means "many injured people", but with a noun, the participle stays fixed: *palju vigastatud inimesi*.

## What the impersonal means (§ 342–343)

Estonian uses the impersonal when the doer is unknown, general, or unimportant. English may use a passive, "they", or "one".

| Example | English |
|---|---|
| *Poes müüakse leiba.* | Bread is sold in the shop. |
| *Eestis juuakse palju kohvi.* | People drink a lot of coffee in Estonia. |
| *Siin ujutakse.* | People swim here. |

Objects follow the usual choice, but a total object appears in the nominative:

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Partial | *Autot pestakse.* "The car is being washed." |
| Total | *Auto pesti puhtaks.* "The car was washed clean." |

Naming the doer with *poolt* + genitive is possible, but natural Estonian usually makes the doer the subject of an active sentence.

## Present impersonal (§ 344–349)

Replace *-tud* with *-takse*, and *-dud* with *-dakse*.

| Participle | Present | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| *õpitud* | *õpitakse* | regular |
| *ostetud* | *ostetakse* | regular |
| *antud* | *antakse* | regular |
| *saadud* | *saadakse* | regular |
| *kuuldud* | *kuuldakse* | regular |
| *tuldud* | *tullakse* | *l + l* |
| *mindud* | *minnakse* | *n + n* |
| *pandud* | *pannakse* | *n + n* |
| *oldud* | *ollakse* | *l + l* |
| *surdud* | *surrakse* | *r + r* |
| *toodud* | *tuuakse* | vowel change, no *d* |
| *joodud* | *juuakse* | vowel change, no *d* |
| *söödud* | *süüakse* | vowel change, no *d* |
| *viidud* | *viiakse* | no *d* |
| *müüdud* | *müüakse* | no *d* |
| *tehtud* | *tehakse* | no *t* |
| *nähtud* | *nähakse* | no *t* |

The negative is *ei* plus the participle stem with *-ta* or *-da*, with none of the special changes above:

| Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|
| *õpitakse* | *ei õpita* |
| *tullakse* | *ei tulda* |
| *tuuakse* | *ei tooda* |
| *tehakse* | *ei tehta* |

## Past impersonal (§ 350–352)

| Participle | Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| *õpitud* | *õpiti* | *ei õpitud* |
| *saadud* | *saadi* | *ei saadud* |
| *tuldud* | *tuldi* | *ei tuldud* |
| *söödud* | *söödi* | *ei söödud* |
| *tehtud* | *tehti* | *ei tehtud* |

The negative past simply uses *ei* plus the participle.

## All the forms of *kutsuma* (§ 353–358)

| Form | Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| Present | *kutsutakse* | *ei kutsuta* |
| Past | *kutsuti* | *ei kutsutud* |
| Perfect | *on kutsutud* | *ei ole kutsutud* |
| Pluperfect | *oli kutsutud* | *ei olnud kutsutud* |
| Conditional | *kutsutaks* | *ei kutsutaks* |
| Past conditional | *oleks kutsutud* | *ei oleks kutsutud* |
| Oblique | *kutsutavat* | *ei kutsutavat* |
| Past oblique | *olevat kutsutud* | *ei olevat kutsutud* |
| Command | *kutsutagu* | *ärgu kutsutagu* |
| Past command | *olgu kutsutud* | *ärgu olgu kutsutud* |

Two other forms complete the set: the passive infinitive *kutsutama* and the participle *kutsutav*. The infinitive is used, for example, after *peaks*: *Seda peaks kontrollitama.* "This should be checked."

## Useful phrases

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| *Kuidas seda eesti keeles öeldakse?* | How do you say this in Estonian? |
| *Kuidas seda kirjutatakse?* | How is this written? |
| *Kas siin räägitakse inglise keelt?* | Is English spoken here? |
| *Räägitakse, et ...* | They say that ... |
| *Hästi tehtud!* | Well done! |
| *Avatud / Suletud* | Open / Closed (signs) |
