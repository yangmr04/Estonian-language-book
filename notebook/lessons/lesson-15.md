# Lesson 15: The Short Illative

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 86 · § 85–91 · [← Previous](lesson-14.md) · [Next →](lesson-16.md)

## Key ideas

- Many common words have a second, shorter illative besides *-sse*.
- Short forms end in *-de/-te*, *-he/-hu*, or just a vowel.
- Clothing and body parts use the inner cases: put on, have on, take off.
- Personal pronouns take inner case endings, with reduced plural forms.

## Forms of the short illative (§ 85–88)

**Ending in *-de* or *-te***

| Nominative | Genitive | Long | Short | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| noor | noore | nooresse | noorde | young |
| tuul | tuule | tuulesse | tuulde | wind |
| mesi | mee | meesse | mette | honey |
| süsi | söe | söesse | sütte | coal |

**Ending in *-he* or *-hu*.** This is a very small group: *pea* → *pähe* "into the head", *suu* → *suhu* "into the mouth", *soo* → *sohu* "into the swamp".

**Ending in a vowel.** The middle of the word is pronounced extra long, and the spelling often shows a strong consonant or a double one.

| Nominative | Genitive | Long | Short | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| park | pargi | pargisse | parki | park |
| mets | metsa | metsasse | metsa | forest |
| kott | koti | kotisse | kotti | bag |
| kapp | kapi | kapisse | kappi | cupboard |
| pesa | pesa | pesasse | pessa | nest |
| lumi | lume | lumesse | lumme | snow |
| jalg | jala | jalasse | jalga | leg, foot |
| selg | selja | seljasse | selga | back |

The final vowel usually matches the genitive, but not always:

| Nominative | Genitive | Short illative |
|---|---|---|
| aed | aia | aeda |
| mägi | mäe | mäkke |
| sõda | sõja | sõtta |
| rida | rea | ritta |
| kodu | kodu | koju |

Where a short form exists, it is often the everyday choice: *Ma lähen koju.* An adjective can also be short: *Ma lähen suurde parki.* "I'm going to the big park."

| Pattern | Examples |

## Wearing and placing (§ 90)

The body part goes in the illative for putting on, the inessive for wearing, and the elative for taking off.

| Item | Put on | Have on | Take off |
|---|---|---|---|
| kübar "hat" | pähe | peas | peast |
| kampsun "sweater" | selga | seljas | seljast |
| lips "necktie" | kaela | kaelas | kaelast |
| kõrvarõngas "earring" | kõrva | kõrvas | kõrvast |
| kindad "gloves" | kätte | käes | käest |
| sokid "socks" | jalga | jalas | jalast |

*Pane kampsun selga!* "Put your sweater on!" · *Kübar on peas.* "The hat is on." · *Võta lips kaelast!* "Take off your tie!"

The same three-way pattern appears with a few other places:

| Where | Into | In | Out of |
|---|---|---|---|
| at the table | Palun lauda! | Pere on lauas. | Ta tõuseb lauast. |
| the ground | istu maha | Võti on maas. | Tõsta kott maast! |
| a hill | Tee viib mäkke. | | Suusatame mäest alla. |

## Personal pronouns (§ 91)

The endings go on the genitive form. The plural is shortened before the ending.

| Pronoun | Illative | Inessive | Elative |
|---|---|---|---|
| mina | minusse (musse) | minus (mus) | minust (must) |
| sina | sinusse (susse) | sinus (sus) | sinust (sust) |
| tema | temasse (tasse) | temas (tas) | temast (tast) |
| meie | meisse | meis | meist |
| teie | teisse | teis | teist |
| nemad | nendesse (neisse) | nendes (neis) | nendest (neist) |

*Ma usun sinusse.* "I believe in you." · *Mis meist saab?* "What will become of us?"
