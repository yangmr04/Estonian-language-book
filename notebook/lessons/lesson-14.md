# Lesson 14: The Inner Locative Cases

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 79 · § 74–84 · [← Previous](lesson-13.md) · [Next →](lesson-15.md)

## Key ideas

- Three cases describe the inside of something: into it, in it, out of it.
- All three endings are added to the genitive.
- These cases also have many non-spatial uses, especially the elative.
- An adjective takes the same case ending as its noun.

## The three cases at a glance (§ 74–76, 78, 80)

| Case | Ending | Meaning | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Illative | -sse | into | kuhu? kellesse? millesse? |
| Inessive | -s | in, inside | kus? kelles? milles? |
| Elative | -st | out of | kust? kellest? millest? |

| Nominative | Genitive | Illative | Inessive | Elative | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| kohvik | kohviku | kohvikusse | kohvikus | kohvikust | café |
| aed | aia | aiasse | aias | aiast | garden |
| kott | koti | kotisse | kotis | kotist | bag |
| Tallinn | Tallinna | Tallinnasse | Tallinnas | Tallinnast | Tallinn |
| Tartu | Tartu | Tartusse | Tartus | Tartust | Tartu |

A shorter illative exists for words with a long genitive in *-se*, where that syllable can drop (§ 76):

| Nominative | Genitive | Full illative | Shortened |
|---|---|---|---|
| inimene | inimese | inimesesse | inimesse |
| hobune | hobuse | hobusesse | hobusse |
| kauplus | kaupluse | kauplusesse | kauplusse |

## Illative and inessive beyond place (§ 77, 79)

English often uses another preposition here, or none at all.

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| Ma usun headusesse. | I believe in goodness. |
| Ta armub Tallinnasse. | She is falling in love with Tallinn. |
| See ei puutu asjasse. | That's beside the point. |
| Ta on heas tujus. | He is in a good mood. |
| Kiri on soome keeles. | The letter is in Finnish. |
| Ma olen segaduses. | I am confused. |

## Other uses of the elative (§ 81–83)

| Use | Example | English |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Kauss on klaasist. | The bowl is made of glass. |
| Topic | Räägi oma perest. | Tell me about your family. |
| Topic | Mida sa sellest filmist arvad? | What do you think of this film? |
| *aru saama* | Ma saan sinust aru. | I understand you. |
| *osa võtma* | Ma võtan kursusest osa. | I am taking part in the course. |
| *lugu pidama* | Ma pean muusikast lugu. | I think highly of music. |
| *maha jääma* | Ära jää bussist maha! | Don't miss the bus! |
| Cause | Ta väriseb külmast. | She is shivering from the cold. |
| *tüdinenud olema* | Olen talvest tüdinenud. | I am tired of winter. |

Note *kodu* "home": *kodunt* means "from home" and keeps it apart from *kodust* "about home". You will also hear *kodust* for "from home".

## Adjectives agree (§ 84)

The adjective repeats the case ending of the noun.

| Illative | Inessive | Elative |
|---|---|---|
| vanasse kohvikusse | vanas kohvikus | vanast kohvikust |
| punasesse kotisse | punases kotis | punasest kotist |

## Useful phrases

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| Kust sa pärit oled? | Where are you from? |
| Ma olen pärit Eestist. | I am from Estonia. |
| Kus sa elad? | Where do you live? |
| Kuhu sa lähed? | Where are you going? |
| Kus on tualett? | Where is the toilet? |
| Ma ei saa aru. | I don't understand. |

## Exercises

**A. Give the illative, inessive and elative (long forms).**

1. kool (gen. kooli)
2. aed (gen. aia)
3. Tartu (gen. Tartu)

**B. Put the word in brackets into the correct case.**

1. Ma lähen ___ (kohvik).
2. Raamat on ___ (kott).
3. Laps tuleb ___ (aed).
4. Me elame ___ (Tallinn).
5. Õpetaja räägib ___ (film, gen. filmi).
6. Kruus on ___ (klaas, gen. klaasi).

**C. Translate into Estonian.**

1. We are in a new café.
2. I am going into an old café.
3. The bag is made of paper (*paber*, gen. *paberi*).
4. We are talking about the weather (*ilm*, gen. *ilma*).
