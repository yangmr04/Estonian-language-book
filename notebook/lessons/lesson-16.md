# Lesson 16: The Outer Locative Cases

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 93 · § 92–103 · [← Previous](lesson-15.md) · [Next →](lesson-17.md)

## Key ideas

- Three more cases describe a surface or surroundings: onto, on, off.
- The endings *-le*, *-l*, *-lt* are added to the genitive.
- The allative also marks the person something goes to.
- The ablative also marks the person something comes from.

## The three cases at a glance (§ 92, 93, 96, 99)

| Case | Ending | Meaning | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Allative | *-le* | onto, to | *kuhu? kellele? millele?* |
| Adessive | *-l* | on, at | *kus? kellel? millel?* |
| Ablative | *-lt* | off, from | *kust? kellelt? millelt?* |

| Nominative | Genitive | Allative | Adessive | Ablative | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *põrand* | *põranda* | *põrandale* | *põrandal* | *põrandalt* | floor |
| *riiul* | *riiuli* | *riiulile* | *riiulil* | *riiulilt* | shelf |
| *diivan* | *diivani* | *diivanile* | *diivanil* | *diivanilt* | sofa |
| *rand* | *ranna* | *rannale* | *rannal* | *rannalt* | beach |
| *saar* | *saare* | *saarele* | *saarel* | *saarelt* | island |
| *Tiit* | *Tiidu* | *Tiidule* | *Tiidul* | *Tiidult* | (a name) |

## What each case does (§ 94–95, 97–98, 100–101)

| Case | Use | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| Allative | onto a surface | *Pane kruus riiulile.* | Put the mug on the shelf. |
| Allative | recipient or addressee | *Anna võti Tiidule.* | Give the key to Tiit. |
| Allative | recipient or addressee | *Ma helistan õpetajale.* | I am calling the teacher. |
| Adessive | on a surface or place | *Kass magab diivanil.* | The cat is sleeping on the sofa. |
| Adessive | time | *Järgmisel nädalal ma puhkan.* | Next week I am resting. |
| Ablative | off a surface or place | *Lapsed tulevad rannalt.* | The children are coming from the beach. |
| Ablative | source person | *Küsi õpetajalt abi.* | Ask the teacher for help. |
| Ablative | source person | *Ma ostan naabrilt auto.* | I am buying a car from the neighbor. |

The adessive's use for time is the one from Lesson 13. It also expresses possession, which is the topic of Lesson 17.

## Adjectives agree (§ 102)

As with the inner cases, the adjective repeats the ending:

- *Istu pehmele diivanile.* "Sit on the soft sofa."
- *Me elame väikesel saarel.* "We live on a small island."
- *Ta tuleb pikalt jalutuskäigult.* "She is coming back from a long walk."

## Personal pronouns (§ 103)

| Pronoun | Allative | Adessive | Ablative |
|---|---|---|---|
| *mina* | *minule, mulle* | *minul, mul* | *minult, mult* |
| *sina* | *sinule, sulle* | *sinul, sul* | *sinult, sult* |
| *tema* | *temale, talle* | *temal, tal* | *temalt, talt* |
| *meie* | *meile* | *meil* | *meilt* |
| *teie* | *teile* | *teil* | *teilt* |
| *nemad* | *nendele, neile* | *nendel, neil* | *nendelt, neilt* |

*Anna talle võti.* "Give him the key."

*Meile*, *teile* and *neile* can also mean "to our place", "to your place", "to their place": *Õhtul tuleme teile.* "We'll come to your place this evening."

## Exercises

**A. Put the word in brackets into the correct case.**

1. *Pane kott ___ (põrand).*
2. *Võti on ___ (riiul).*
3. *Võta raamat ___ (diivan).*
4. *Me sõidame ___ (saar).*
5. *Anna raha ___ (Tiit).*
6. *Küsi ___ (õpetaja).*

**B. Give the short pronoun form.**

1. *Anna raamat ___* (*mina*, to).
2. *Küsi ___* (*tema*, from).
3. *Ma helistan ___* (*sina*, to).
4. *Kass on ___* (*nemad*, at/on).

**C. Translate into Estonian.**

1. The children are on the beach.
2. Sit on the sofa!
3. I am calling them.
4. Tomorrow we'll come to your place (plural you).
