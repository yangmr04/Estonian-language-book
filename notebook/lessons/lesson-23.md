# Lesson 23: The Past Tense

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 143 · § 154–164 · [← Previous](lesson-22.md) · [Next →](lesson-24.md)

## Key ideas

- The simple past is built on the *-ma* stem, not the present stem.
- Most verbs use *-si-*. A smaller group uses *-i-*.
- The "you" singular and "they" forms look the same.

## The -si- past (§ 154–157)

Drop *-ma* and add the endings.

| Person | Ending | *ujuma* "to swim" | *magama* "to sleep" |
|---|---|---|---|
| ma | -sin | ujusin | magasin |
| sa | -sid | ujusid | magasid |
| ta | -s | ujus | magas |
| me | -sime | ujusime | magasime |
| te | -site | ujusite | magasite |
| nad | -sid | ujusid | magasid |

Because the past follows the *-ma* stem, it may not look like the present or the *-da* form:

| Present | *-da* | *-ma* | Past |
|---|---|---|---|
| kannan | kanda | kandma | kandsin |
| õpin | õppida | õppima | õppisin |
| hoian | hoida | hoidma | hoidsin |
| hüppan | hüpata | hüppama | hüppasin |

*Eile ma ujusin ja puhkasin.* "Yesterday I swam and rested."

## Third person after a consonant stem (§ 158)

If the *-ma* stem ends in a consonant, the third-person singular has *-is*:

| Verb | ma | ta |
|---|---|---|
| kandma | kandsin | kand**is** |
| ostma | ostsin | ost**is** |
| kuulma | kuulsin | kuul**is** |

After a short vowel, a single *t* or *p* doubles in this form:

| Verb | ma | ta |
|---|---|---|
| jätma | jätsin | jä**tt**is |
| petma | petsin | pe**tt**is |

## Stems ending in s; *minema* (§ 159–160)

A stem ending in *s* does not add another *s*. The third-person singular still has *-is*.

| Person | *tõusma* "to rise" | *minema* "to go" |
|---|---|---|
| ma | tõusin | läksin |
| sa | tõusid | läksid |
| ta | tõusis | läks |
| me | tõusime | läksime |
| te | tõusite | läksite |
| nad | tõusid | läksid |

*Minema* takes its past from the same root as *lähen*.

## The -i- past (§ 161–163)

Here the third-person singular is simply *-i*.

| Person | Ending | *panema* "to put" | *sööma* "to eat" |
|---|---|---|---|
| ma | -in | panin | sõin |
| sa | -id | panid | sõid |
| ta | -i | pani | sõi |
| me | -ime | panime | sõime |
| te | -ite | panite | sõite |
| nad | -id | panid | sõid |

Two groups use this pattern:

| Group | Verbs | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Two-syllable stems in *-e-* | olema, tulema, tegema, nägema, panema, pesema, surema | *-e-* is replaced: olin, tulin, tegin, nägin |
| Long vowel stems | saama, jääma | long vowel shortens: sain, jäin |
| | jooma, sööma, tooma, lööma, looma | *oo/öö* becomes *õ*: jõin, sõin, tõin, lõin, lõin |

Not every long-vowel verb belongs here. *Käima*, *viima* and *müüma* use *-si-*: *käisin*, *viisin*, *müüsin*.

## Two verbs with a choice (§ 164)

| Verb | Past | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| pidama | pidasin | held, kept |
| pidama | pidin | had to |
| laskma | lasksin or lasin | let; shot (either form, either meaning) |

*Pidin täna vara tõusma.* "I had to get up early today." *Me pidasime eile pidu.* "We held a party yesterday."
