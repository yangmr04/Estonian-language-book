# Lesson 10: Diminutives

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 56 · § 54–57 · [← Previous](lesson-09.md) · [Next →](lesson-11.md)

## Key ideas

- A diminutive adds *-ke* or *-kene* to the genitive.
- It suggests smallness, affection, or both.
- An adjective beside a diminutive stays ordinary.
- The genitive of every diminutive ends in *-kese*.

## Making a diminutive (§ 54)

Diminutives are typical of children's language, folk song and poetry. Start from the genitive. The two endings are interchangeable.

| Nominative | Genitive | Diminutive | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| *kass* | *kassi* | *kassike(ne)* | kitty |
| *koer* | *koera* | *koerake(ne)* | doggie |
| *kala* | *kala* | *kalake(ne)* | little fish |
| *lill* | *lille* | *lilleke(ne)* | little flower |
| *lind* | *linnu* | *linnuke(ne)* | birdie |
| *maja* | *maja* | *majake(ne)* | little house |
| *tuba* | *toa* | *toake(ne)* | little room |
| *õde* | *õe* | *õeke(ne)* | little sister |

Since the genitive is the base, any stem change from Lesson 8 appears in the diminutive as well.

## Adjectives and diminutives (§ 55)

An adjective used on its own, as a term of address, can take the suffix: *mu kallike* "my dear one" (*kallis*, gen. *kalli*).

When it stands in front of a diminutive noun, the adjective stays plain:

| Correct | Not used |
|---|---|
| *mu kallis kassike* "my dear kitty" | *mu kallike kassike* |

## Words that are diminutive only in form (§ 56)

Many everyday words end in *-ke* without any sense of smallness or affection today.

| Word | English |
|---|---|
| *väike* | small |
| *pisike* | tiny |
| *tilluke* | teeny |
| *lühike* | short |
| *päike* | sun |

## The genitive (§ 57)

Both *-ke* and *-kene* become *-kese*, on true diminutives and frozen words alike.

| Nominative | Genitive |
|---|---|
| *kassike(ne)* | *kassikese* |
| *linnuke(ne)* | *linnukese* |
| *väike* | *väikese* |
| *päike* | *päikese* |

Example: *kassikese kauss* "the kitty's bowl".

| Estonian | English |

1. *koer (gen. koera)*
2. kala (gen. kala)
3. lind (gen. linnu)
4. tuba (gen. toa)
5. maja (gen. maja)

**B. Give the genitive.**

1. *kassike*
2. *väike*
3. *päike*
4. *linnuke*
5. *kalakene*

**C. Translate into Estonian.**

1. my dear kitty
2. Sleep, birdie!
3. the kitty's bowl
4. beside the small house
5. Dear one, come home!
