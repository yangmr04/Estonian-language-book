# Lesson 20: "With" and "Without"

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 119 · § 124–129 · [← Previous](lesson-19.md) · [Next →](lesson-21.md)

## Key ideas

- The comitative *-ga* means "with". The abessive *-ta* means "without".
- Both endings are added to the genitive.
- *Ilma* can be placed before an abessive for clarity.
- Adjectives do not take these endings. Only the last noun does.

## The two cases (§ 124–125)

| Case | Ending | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| Comitative | *-ga* | *kellega? millega?* |
| Abessive | *-ta* | *kelleta? milleta?* |

| Nominative | Genitive | Comitative | Abessive | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *naaber* | *naabri* | *naabriga* | *naabrita* | neighbor |
| *ema* | *ema* | *emaga* | *emata* | mother |
| *nuga* | *noa* | *noaga* | *noata* | knife |
| *suhkur* | *suhkru* | *suhkruga* | *suhkruta* | sugar |
| *võti* | *võtme* | *võtmega* | *võtmeta* | key |
| *kaart* | *kaardi* | *kaardiga* | *kaardita* | card |

## Uses of the comitative (§ 124)

| Use | Example | English |
|---|---|---|
| Company | *Ma lähen emaga.* | I'm going with my mother. |
| Tool | *Lõika noaga.* | Cut with a knife. |
| Transport | *Ma sõidan rongiga.* | I'm going by train. |
| Description | *prillidega mees* | the man with glasses |
| Time taken | *Ma teen selle kahe päevaga.* | I'll do it in two days. |
| *rahul olema* | *Olen ilmaga rahul.* | I'm happy with the weather. |
| *sarnanema* | *Mari sarnaneb vanaemaga.* | Mari looks like her grandmother. |

## Uses of the abessive (§ 125–126)

- *Kohv suhkruta.* "Coffee without sugar."
- *Võtmeta me sisse ei saa.* "We can't get in without a key."
- *Rahata on raske.* "It's hard without money."

The preposition *ilma* "without" often comes before the abessive, and it does not change the meaning: *ilma rahata*, *ilma suhkruta*.

*Ära mine välja ilma mütsita!* "Don't go out without a hat!"

## Personal pronouns (§ 127)

The endings go on the long genitive. There are no short forms here.

| Pronoun | Comitative | Abessive |
|---|---|---|
| *mina* | *minuga* | *minuta* |
| *sina* | *sinuga* | *sinuta* |
| *tema* | *temaga* | *temata* |
| *meie* | *meiega* | *meieta* |
| *teie* | *teiega* | *teieta* |
| *nemad* | *nendega* | *nendeta* |

*Tule minuga!* "Come with me!" · *Me ei lähe ilma temata.* "We won't go without him."

## Adjectives and series (§ 128–129)

This is different from the locative cases. The adjective stays in the plain genitive and only the noun takes the ending.

| Nominative | Genitive | With the ending |
|---|---|---|
| *sõbralik naaber* | *sõbraliku naabri* | *sõbraliku naabriga* |
| *punane auto* | *punase auto* | *punase autoga* |
| *külm piim* | *külma piima* | *külma piimata* |
| *uus võti* | *uue võtme* | *uue võtmeta* |

In a list, the ending usually goes only on the last noun, and the others stay in the genitive:

- *Ma lähen kolleegi ja naabriga.* "I'm going with a colleague and a neighbor."
- *Ma söön leiba või ja juustuga.* "I eat bread with butter and cheese."
- *Kohv suhkru ja piimata.* "Coffee without sugar or milk."

## Useful phrases

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| *Hea meelega!* | Gladly! |
| *Suure rõõmuga!* | With great pleasure! |
| *Tule meiega!* | Come with us! |
| *Kohvi piimaga, palun.* | Coffee with milk, please. |
| *Ilma suhkruta, palun.* | Without sugar, please. |
| *Kas saab kaardiga maksta?* | Can I pay by card? |

## Exercises

**A. Give the comitative and abessive.**

1. *raha (gen. raha)*
2. *kolleeg (gen. kolleegi)*
3. *lusikas (gen. lusika)*
4. *piim (gen. piima)*

**B. Give the correct form.**

1. Ma sõidan ___ (uus auto, with).
2. Tee ___ (suhkur, without).
3. *Ta räägib ___ (sõbralik naaber, with).*
4. Ära mine välja ilma ___ (soe mantel; gen. *sooja mantli*).

**C. Translate into Estonian.**

1. Come with us!
2. I won't go without you (one friend).
3. with mother and father
4. I am writing with a pen (*pastakas*, gen. *pastaka*).
5. We are going by train.
