# Lesson 34: Commands for Every Person

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 248 · § 301–317 · [← Previous](lesson-33.md) · [Next →](lesson-35.md)

## Key ideas

- The singular "you" command comes from the present stem. All other commands come from the *-da* infinitive.
- The endings have a *g* form and a *k* form: *-ge/-ke*, *-gem/-kem*, *-gu/-ku*.
- Each negative command has its own form of *ära*.
- Only "I" has no command form.

## "You" plural or polite (§ 301–308)

A command to one familiar person is the bare present stem: *Tule!* A command to several people, or a polite one, uses *-ge* or *-ke* after the stem of the *-da* infinitive.

| The *-da* infinitive | Ending | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| ends in *-da* | **-ge** | *kirjutada → kirjutage, õppida → õppige, jääda → jääge* |
| ends in *-ta* | **-ke** | *kuulata → kuulake, aidata → aidake, osta → ostke* |
| ends in *-a*, after a vowel, *l*, *n* or *r* | **-ge** | *olla → olge, tulla → tulge, panna → pange, minna → minge* |
| ends in *-a*, after *d*, *h* or *t* | **-ke** | *anda → andke, hoida → hoidke, näha → nähke, võtta → võtke* |

Double consonants shorten before the ending: *tulla* → *tulge*, *võtta* → *võtke*.

A few short verbs use the present stem instead: *tooge*, *looge*, *jooge*, *sööge*, *lööge*.

For the negative, put *ärge* in front: *Ärge muretsege!* "Don't worry!" *Ärge unustage võtmeid!* "Don't forget the keys!"

## "Let's" (§ 309–312)

The formal form adds *-gem* or *-kem*. Everyday speech normally uses the plain *me* present.

| Style | Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| Formal | *Alustagem!* | *Ärgem alustagem!* |
| Everyday | *Alustame!* | *Ärme alustame!* |

- The *g/k* choice is the same as for *-ge/-ke*: *laulgem*, *tehkem*.
- The present-stem verbs stay the same: *söögem*.
- The everyday "let's go" is *Lähme!*

## Third-person commands (§ 313–315)

The form with *-gu* or *-ku* is the same for "he", "she" and "they". It gives an indirect order, expresses a wish, or shows resignation.

| Example | English |
|---|---|
| *Ta tulgu homme.* | He should come tomorrow. |
| *Nad oodaku väljas.* | Let them wait outside. |
| *Tehku, mis tahab!* | Let him do what he wants! |
| *Olgu!* | All right! |

In everyday resignation, use *las* with the present: *Las ta magab.* "Let her sleep." *Las poisid mängivad.* "Let the boys play."

For the negative, put *ärgu* before the verb: *Ärgu ta helistagu!* "He'd better not phone!"

## Summary with *võtma, võtta* (§ 316)

| Person | Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| *sina* | *võta!* | *ära võta!* |
| *tema* | *võtku! / las võtab!* | *ärgu võtku!* |
| *meie* (formal) | *võtkem!* | *ärgem võtkem!* |
| *meie* (everyday) | *võtame!* | *ärme võtame!* |
| *teie* | *võtke!* | *ärge võtke!* |
| *nemad* | *võtku! / las võtavad!* | *ärgu võtku!* |

## Forms built on the *-da* infinitive (§ 317)

| Form | *kirjutada* |
|---|---|
| Gerund | *kirjutades* |
| *-nud* participle | *kirjutanud* |
| Negative past | *ma ei kirjutanud* |
| Perfect and pluperfect | *olen kirjutanud, olin kirjutanud* |
| Past conditional | *oleksin kirjutanud* |
| Commands | *kirjutagem, kirjutage, kirjutagu* |

## Useful phrases

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| *Tulge sisse!* | Come in! |
| *Võtke istet!* | Have a seat! |
| *Olge hea!* | Here you are! / Please do! |
| *Vabandage!* | Excuse me! |
| *Ära muretse!* | Don't worry! |
| *Lähme!* | Let's go! |
| *Olgu!* | Okay! |

2. *Laulgem!*
3. *Tehku!*

1. Let's go home!
2. Let him wait.
