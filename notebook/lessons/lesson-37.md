# Lesson 37: Adverbs and Verb Particles

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 283 · § 359–367 · [← Previous](lesson-36.md) · [Next →](lesson-38.md)

## Key ideas

- Some adverbs are basic words. Others come from frozen case forms or suffixes.
- Place adverbs often come in sets of three: to, at, and from.
- Adverbs in *-lt* compare with *-malt*, and those in *-sti* compare with *-mini*.
- A particle can change the meaning of a verb.

## Basic adverbs (§ 359)

These do not come from other words by a regular rule: *täna* "today", *kohe* "right away", *väga* "very", *juba* "already", *alati* "always".

## Adverbs from case forms (§ 360–362)

The ablative of an adjective is a common way to say "in this manner":

| Adjective | Genitive | Adverb |
|---|---|---|
| rõõmus | rõõmsa | rõõmsalt "cheerfully" |
| vaikne | vaikse | vaikselt "quietly" |
| lihtne | lihtsa | lihtsalt "simply" |

Place adverbs have three directions:

| To where | At where | From where | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| siia | siin | siit | here |
| sinna | seal | sealt | there |
| kuhu | kus | kust | where? |
| koju | kodus | kodust | home |
| välja | väljas | väljast | out |
| ette | ees | eest | in front |
| taha | taga | tagant | behind |
| kuhugi | kuskil | kuskilt | somewhere |

Both parts of a compound change: *väljapoole*, *väljaspool*, *väljastpoolt*. *Igale poole, igal pool, igalt poolt* are written as two words.

Other frozen case forms include *õnneks* "luckily", *kahjuks* "unfortunately", and *näiteks* "for example".

An old case survives in a few fixed words that answer "how?": *paljajalu* "barefoot", *kinnisilmi* "with eyes closed".

## Adverbs with suffixes (§ 363)

| Suffix | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| -sti | manner | kenasti, soojasti, hästi "well" |
| -ti | repeated time, manner | reedeti "on Fridays", sügiseti "in autumn", teisiti "otherwise" |
| -li | position | selili "on one's back", külili "on one's side" |
| -ldi | manner | eraldi "separately" |
| -kesi | how many together | kahekesi "the two together", kolmekesi "the three together" |
| -vel, -il | a state | ärkvel "awake", ärevil "agitated" |
| -haaval, -viisi, -moodi | compounds | tükkhaaval "piece by piece", hulgaviisi "in large numbers", teistmoodi "differently" |

*-sti* and *-lt* often mean the same, but not always:

| Form | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| kindlasti | surely | *Ma helistan kindlasti.* |
| kindlalt | firmly | *Ta seisab kindlalt.* |

## Comparing adverbs (§ 364–366)

| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| tugevalt | tugevamalt | kõige tugevamalt |
| vaikselt | vaiksemalt | kõige vaiksemalt |
| kiiresti | kiiremini | kõige kiiremini |
| hästi | paremini | kõige paremini |
| halvasti | halvemini | kõige halvemini |

A small group adds *-m* to the basic word:

| Positive | Comparative |
|---|---|
| vara | varem |
| hilja | hiljem |
| kaua | kauem |
| vähe | vähem |
| palju | rohkem, enam |
| harva | harvem |
| ruttu | rutem |

*Kõige kiiremini jooksis Mari.* "Mari ran the fastest." A comparative alone can sometimes carry the top-degree meaning.

## Verb particles (§ 367)

A particle usually follows the verb and often goes at the end of the clause: *Ma panin ukse kinni.* "I closed the door." Learn the pair as one vocabulary item. Here is a starter set:

| Combination | Meaning |
|---|---|
| kinni panema | to close |
| lahti tegema | to open |
| ära tegema | to get done |
| tagasi tulema | to come back |
| järele mõtlema | to think over |
| läbi lugema | to read through |
| kokku panema | to assemble |
| üle vaatama | to look over |
| sisse lülitama | to switch on |
| välja lülitama | to switch off |

## Useful phrases

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| Kohe tulen! | I'm coming right away! |
| Räägi aeglasemalt, palun! | Speak more slowly, please! |
| Rääkige valjemini, palun! | Please speak louder! |
| Varem või hiljem. | Sooner or later. |
| Mida varem, seda parem. | The sooner, the better. |
| Nii ja naa. | So-so. |

## Exercises

**A. Give the adverb and its comparative.**

1. rõõmus (use *-lt*)
2. kiire (use *-sti*)
3. vaikne (use *-lt*)
4. hea
5. halb

**B. Fill in the right word of the set.**

1. Tule ___! (here, movement)
2. Ma elan ___. (there, location)
3. ___ sa tuled? (where from)
4. Ta on ___. (at home)
5. Mine ___! (outside, movement)

**C. Translate into Estonian.**

1. I closed the window.
2. Read the text through!
3. I'll think it over.
4. We come back on Fridays.
