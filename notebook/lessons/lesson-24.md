# Lesson 24: The Negative Past, -nud and Compound Tenses

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 151 · § 165–173 · [← Previous](lesson-23.md) · [Next →](lesson-25.md)

## Key ideas

- The negative past is *ei* + the *-nud* participle, the same for every person.
- The *-nud* participle comes from the *-da* infinitive.
- Compound tenses combine a form of *olema* with *-nud*.

## Negative past (§ 165)

| Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|
| *ma ujusin* | *ma ei ujunud* |
| *sa kirjutasid* | *sa ei kirjutanud* |
| *ta ostis* | *ta ei ostnud* |
| *me olime* | *me ei olnud* |
| *nad tulid* | *nad ei tulnud* |

There is no personal ending on the negative form.

## Forming -nud (§ 166–168)

Replace the *-da*, *-ta* or *-a* ending of the *-da* infinitive with *-nud*.

| *-da* form | *-nud* | English |
|---|---|---|
| *ujuda* | *ujunud* | swum |
| *magada* | *maganud* | slept |
| *kuulata* | *kuulanud* | listened |
| *pesta* | *pesnud* | washed |
| *kanda* | *kandnud* | carried |
| *saada* | *saanud* | got |
| *olla* | *olnud* | been |
| *surra* | *surnud* | died |
| *jätta* | *jätnud* | left |

Double *l*, *r* and *tt* become single before *-nud*. The double *n* in *panna* stays: *pannud*.

Some participles must be learned individually:

| *-da* form | *-nud* | | *-da* form | *-nud* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *näha* | *näinud* |  | *süüa* | *söönud* |
| *teha* | *teinud* |  | *juua* | *joonud* |
| *minna* | *läinud* |  | *tuua* | *toonud* |
| *joosta* | *jooksnud* |  | *lüüa* | *löönud* |

*-nud* can also act as an adjective: *kadunud võti* "a lost key", *külmunud järv* "a frozen lake".

In speech and verse, *-nud* may shorten to *-nd*: *väsind* for *väsinud*.

## Present perfect (§ 169–170)

Present of *olema* + *-nud*. In set sayings and poetry, *olema* can be left out.

| Person | Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| *ma* | *olen kirjutanud* | *ei ole / pole kirjutanud* |
| *sa* | *oled kirjutanud* | *ei ole / pole kirjutanud* |
| *ta* | *on kirjutanud* | *ei ole / pole kirjutanud* |
| *me* | *oleme kirjutanud* | *ei ole / pole kirjutanud* |
| *te* | *olete kirjutanud* | *ei ole / pole kirjutanud* |
| *nad* | *on kirjutanud* | *ei ole / pole kirjutanud* |

*Kas sa oled Riias käinud?* "Have you been to Riga?"

## Past perfect (§ 171–172)

Past of *olema* + *-nud*. It marks an action finished before another past point.

| Person | Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| *ma* | *olin kirjutanud* | *ei olnud / polnud kirjutanud* |
| *sa* | *olid kirjutanud* | *ei olnud / polnud kirjutanud* |
| *ta* | *oli kirjutanud* | *ei olnud / polnud kirjutanud* |

The plural works the same way (*olime*, *olite*, *olid*).

## Conditional perfect (§ 173)

Present conditional of *olema* + *-nud*, for what would have happened.

*Kui ma oleksin aega leidnud, oleksin sulle helistanud.* "If I had found time, I would have called you."

The *kui* can be dropped by putting the verb first: *Oleksin ma sellest kuulnud, oleksin sulle helistanud.*

*Võima* in this form means "could have": *Oleksid võinud helistada.* "You could have called."

## Useful phrases

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| *Ma ei teadnud.* | I didn't know. |
| *Mis on juhtunud?* | What has happened? |
| *Ma olen väsinud.* | I'm tired. |
| *Ma pole veel söönud.* | I haven't eaten yet. |
| *Kas sa oled Eestis käinud?* | Have you been to Estonia? |

2. *Ta ostis leiba.*
3. *Nad läksid koju.*
4. *Me nägime filmi.*
5. *Sa sõid.*

**B. Give the *-nud* participle.**

1. *kuulata*
2. *tulla*
3. *teha*
4. *juua*
5. *leida*
6. *panna*

**C. Translate into Estonian.**

1. We have not slept.
2. She had already gone.
3. I would have come.
4. Have you (one friend) read this letter?
