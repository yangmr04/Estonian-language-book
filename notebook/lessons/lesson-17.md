# Lesson 17: "To Have" and Spatial Postpositions

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 100 · § 104–108 · [← Previous](lesson-16.md) · [Next →](lesson-18.md)

## Key ideas

- Estonian has no verb "to have". It uses the adessive of the owner plus *on*.
- Postpositions can replace locative cases for clarity or emphasis.
- Spatial postpositions come in three forms: to, at, from.
- Many of them are old case forms of nouns.

## Adessive + *on* (§ 104)

| Type | Example | English |
|---|---|---|
| Statement | Mul on koer. | I have a dog. |
| Noun as owner | Õpetajal on uus auto. | The teacher has a new car. |
| Question | Kellel on võti? | Who has the key? |
| Negative | Mul ei ole autot. | I don't have a car. |
| Negative, short | Meil pole koera. | We don't have a dog. |

In a negative sentence the thing owned is in the partitive. This is covered in Lesson 27.

Feelings and states follow the same pattern. See the phrases below.

## Replacing a case with a postposition (§ 105)

The noun goes into the genitive and the postposition expresses the direction.

| Case form | Postposition form | English |
|---|---|---|
| kotisse | koti sisse | into the bag |
| kotis | koti sees | in the bag |
| kotist | koti seest | out of the bag |
| riiulile | riiuli peale | onto the shelf |
| riiulil | riiuli peal | on the shelf |
| riiulilt | riiuli pealt | off the shelf |

- *Võti on koti sees.* "The key is inside the bag."
- *Ma mõtlen puhkuse peale.* "I'm thinking about my vacation."

*Käes* and *käest* can replace an adessive or ablative for a person: *Võti on Tiidu käes.* "Tiit has the key." · *Ma küsin Mari käest.* "I'll ask Mari."

## Three-way spatial postpositions (§ 106–107)

Each postposition answers *kuhu?*, *kus?* or *kust?*, and takes the genitive.

| Meaning | *kuhu?* | *kus?* | *kust?* |
|---|---|---|---|
| at, by (a person, thing) | juurde | juures | juurest |
| in front of | ette | ees | eest |
| behind | taha | taga | tagant |
| under | alla | all | alt |
| beside | kõrvale | kõrval | kõrvalt |
| at the edge of | äärde | ääres | äärest |
| between | vahele | vahel | vahelt |

| Direction | Example | English |
|---|---|---|
| To | Ma lähen arsti juurde. | I am going to the doctor's. |
| At | Ma olen arsti juures. | I am at the doctor's. |
| From | Ma tulen arsti juurest. | I am coming from the doctor's. |
| To | Laps ronib voodi alla. | The child crawls under the bed. |
| At | Laps on voodi all. | The child is under the bed. |
| From | Laps tuleb voodi alt välja. | The child comes out from under the bed. |

## Where they come from (§ 108)

Several postpositions are frozen case forms of nouns, often body parts or physical things:

| Noun | Postposition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| pea "head" | peal | laua peal "on the table" |
| juur "root" | juures | maja juures "by the house" |
| äär "edge" | ääres | jõe ääres "by the river" |
| kõrv "ear" | kõrval | ukse kõrval "beside the door" |
| ots "end, tip" | otsas | Kass on katuse otsas. "The cat is up on the roof." |
| käsi "hand" | käes | päikese käes "in the sun" |

## Useful phrases

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| Mul on kiire. | I'm in a hurry. |
| Mul on külm. | I'm cold. |
| Mul on kahju. | I'm sorry. |
| Mul on hea meel. | I'm glad. |
| Sul on õigus. | You're right. |
| Kas sul on aega? | Do you have time? |
| Mul pole aega. | I don't have time. |

## Exercises

**A. Translate into Estonian.**

1. I have a cat.
2. Mari has a sister.
3. Do you (one friend) have a car?
4. Tiit doesn't have a car.

**B. Fill in the postposition.**

1. Koer jookseb auto ___. (to behind)
2. Koer on auto ___. (behind)
3. Koer tuleb auto ___ välja. (from behind)
4. Ma lähen arsti ___. (to)
5. Raamat on laua ___. (under)
6. Istu minu ___! (to beside)

**C. Rewrite with a postposition.**

1. Raha on kotis.
2. Pane kruus riiulile.
3. Võta kruus riiulilt.
