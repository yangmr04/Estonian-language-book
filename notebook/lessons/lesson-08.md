# Lesson 8: Stem Changes, Possessives and Compounds

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 46 · § 42–51 · [← Previous](lesson-07.md) · [Next →](lesson-09.md)

## Key ideas

- A word's stem often changes between the nominative and the genitive.
- Long sounds shorten, *p, t, k* soften, and consonants can vanish.
- The genitive of a personal pronoun is the possessive, and it never changes.
- Compounds start with either a nominative or a genitive word.

## Stem changes in the genitive (§ 42–48)

Each row shows a nominative and its genitive.

| § | Change | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 42 | Double stop becomes single: *pp > p*, *tt > t*, *kk > k* | *supp, supi* "soup"; *kott, koti* "bag"; *pakk, paki* "package" |
| 43 | *p > b*, *t > d*, *k > g* | *tulp, tulbi* "tulip"; *part, pardi* "duck"; *kurk, kurgi* "cucumber" |
| 44 | A consonant turns into another type: *b > v*, *d > j*, *g > j* | *haab, haava* "aspen"; *pada, paja* "pot"; *märg, märja* "wet"; *nälg, nälja* "hunger" |
| 44 | Diphthong with *e* simplified | *aeg, aja* "time"; *aed, aia* "garden" |
| 45 | A consonant disappears | *raud, raua* "iron"; *pood, poe* "shop"; *turg, turu* "market"; *lõug, lõua* "chin"; *roht, rohu* "grass, medicine"; *kask, kase* "birch" |
| 46 | Assimilation: *nd > nn*, *ld > ll*, *rd > rr* | *tund, tunni* "hour"; *kuld, kulla* "gold"; *kord, korra* "occasion, time" |
| 46 | Assimilation to a single sound after a long vowel | *keeld, keelu* "ban" |
| 46 | The reverse direction: genitive is stronger | *tunne, tunde* "feeling"; *hinne, hinde* "grade"; *mõte, mõtte* "thought" |
| 47 | Consonant disappears and a vowel changes | *nuga, noa* "knife"; *pidu, peo* "party"; *rida, rea* "row"; *mägi, mäe* "hill" |
| 48 | More irregular patterns | *aken, akna* "window"; *naaber, naabri* "neighbour"; *käsi, käe* "hand"; *vesi, vee* "water"; *uus, uue* "new" |

The rules explain the changes after the fact, but cannot always predict them. Treat the nominative and genitive as a pair to memorise.

## Possessive pronouns (§ 49)

| Personal pronoun | Possessive | Short form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| mina (ma) | minu | mu | my |
| sina (sa) | sinu | su | your (sg.) |
| tema (ta) | tema | ta | his, her |
| meie (me) | meie | me | our |
| teie (te) | teie | te | your (pl., polite) |
| nemad (nad) | nende | none | their |

The possessive stays the same whatever follows:

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| *minu kass* | my cat |
| *minu kassi nimi* | my cat's name |
| *nende maja* | their house |
| *meie naabri nuga* | our neighbour's knife |

## Compound words (§ 50–51)

The last word carries the case endings, and the first word is fixed.

| First word | Compound | Parts | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | *raudtee* | raud + tee | railway |
| Nominative | *väikelinn* | väike + linn | small town |
| Nominative | *jalgratas* | jalg + ratas | bicycle |
| Nominative | *noormees* | noor + mees | young man |
| Genitive | *raamatupood* | raamatu + pood | bookshop |
| Genitive | *kohvitass* | kohvi + tass | coffee cup |
| Genitive | *hommikusöök* | hommiku + söök | breakfast |
| Genitive | *nädalavahetus* | nädala + vahetus | weekend |
