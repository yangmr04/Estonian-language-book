# Lesson 11: The Nominative Plural

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 61 · § 58–60 · [← Previous](lesson-10.md) · [Next →](lesson-12.md)

## Key ideas

- The plural subject form is the genitive singular plus *-d*.
- Any stem change in the genitive carries into the plural.
- An adjective in front of a plural noun is plural too.
- An adjective after *on* is plural when the subject is plural.

## Forming the plural (§ 58)

Start from the genitive singular, never from the nominative. The genitive always ends in a vowel, so *-d* attaches directly.

| Nominative sg. | Genitive sg. | Nominative pl. | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| *raamat* | *raamatu* | *raamatud* | book |
| *kass* | *kassi* | *kassid* | cat |
| *poiss* | *poisi* | *poisid* | boy |
| *tütar* | *tütre* | *tütred* | daughter |
| *jõgi* | *jõe* | *jõed* | river |
| *tuba* | *toa* | *toad* | room |
| *hea* | *hea* | *head* | good |
| *kõrge* | *kõrge* | *kõrged* | high, tall |
| *punane* | *punase* | *punased* | red |
| *puhas* | *puhta* | *puhtad* | clean |

A plural subject takes a plural verb: *Kassid magavad.* "The cats are sleeping."

## Plural adjectives (§ 59–60)

The adjective matches the noun in both positions.

| Position | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Before the noun | *punane õun* | *punased õunad* |
| Before the noun | *kõrge mägi* | *kõrged mäed* |
| After *on* | *Õun on punane.* | *Õunad on punased.* |
| After *on* | *Tuba on puhas.* | *Toad on puhtad.* |

This also applies when the subject is a plural pronoun: *Me oleme rõõmsad.* "We are happy."

## Useful phrases

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| *Mis ilm täna on?* | What's the weather like today? |
| *Kui ilus ilm!* | What lovely weather! |
| *Täna on külm.* | It's cold today. |
| *Õues on soe.* | It's warm outside. |
| *Tuul on tugev.* | The wind is strong. |

## Exercises

**A. Give the nominative plural. The genitive singular is in brackets.**

1. auto (auto)
2. *õun (õuna)*
3. *sõber (sõbra)*
4. *uks (ukse)*
5. *tütar (tütre)*
6. kala (kala)

**B. Rewrite in the plural.**

1. *Väike kass magab.*
2. *Õun on punane.*
3. *Hea õpetaja räägib.*
4. *Kõrge maja on uus.*
5. Puhas tuba on soe.

**C. Translate into Estonian.**

1. The doors are red.
2. The boys are reading.
3. The good teachers are here.
