# Lesson 12: Numbers and the Partitive

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 66 · § 61–66 · [← Previous](lesson-11.md) · [Next →](lesson-13.md)

## Key ideas

- Numbers above 10 are built from the numbers 1–10 and a few fixed elements.
- *-teist*, *-kümmend* and *-sada* are written joined to their number. Everything else is written apart.
- After *üks* a noun stays in the nominative singular.
- After any other number the noun is in the partitive singular, not the plural.

## 11 to 19 (§ 61)

Add *-teist* to the numbers 1–9. It works like English "-teen", but it also covers 11 and 12. The longer *-teistkümmend* is rare in ordinary speech.

| 11 | 12 | 13 | 14 | 15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *üksteist* | *kaksteist* | *kolmteist* | *neliteist* | *viisteist* |

| 16 | 17 | 18 | 19 |
|---|---|---|---|
| *kuusteist* | *seitseteist* | *kaheksateist* | *üheksateist* |

## Tens, hundreds and above (§ 62–65)

| Pattern | Examples |
|---|---|
| number + *kümmend* | 20 kakskümmend · 40 nelikümmend · 60 kuuskümmend · 90 üheksakümmend |
| tens, then units as a separate word | 34 kolmkümmend neli · 46 nelikümmend kuus · 65 kuuskümmend viis |
| number + *sada* | 100 sada · 200 kakssada · 600 kuussada · 800 kaheksasada |
| *tuhat*, written apart | 1 000 (üks) tuhat · 2 000 kaks tuhat · 40 000 nelikümmend tuhat |
| *miljon*, *miljard* | 1 000 000 (üks) miljon · 3 000 000 kolm miljonit · 2 000 000 000 kaks miljardit |

After a number above one, *miljon* and *miljard* add *-it*, while *tuhat* does not change.

|---|---|
| 215 | *kakssada viisteist* |
| 630 | *kuussada kolmkümmend* |
| 904 | *üheksasada neli* |
| 1 250 | *tuhat kakssada viiskümmend* |
| 2 026 | *kaks tuhat kakskümmend kuus* |

## Nouns after numbers (§ 66)

| Before the noun | Case of the noun | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *üks* | nominative singular | üks päev "one day" |
| 2 and higher | partitive singular | kaks päeva "two days" |
| a compound ending in 1 | partitive singular | kakskümmend üks päeva "21 days" |
| *pool* "half" | partitive singular | pool kilot "half a kilo" |
| *veerand* "quarter" | partitive singular | *veerand kilot* |
| *kolmveerand* "three quarters" | partitive singular | *kolmveerand kilot* |
| *poolteist* "one and a half" | partitive singular | *poolteist kilot* |
| *paar* "a couple" | partitive singular | *paar nädalat* |
| *mitu* "how many, several" | partitive singular | *mitu kuud?* |

The partitive is taught fully in Lesson 27. For now, learn it with each noun:

| Nominative | Partitive | English |
|---|---|---|
| *päev* | *päeva* | day |
| *nädal* | *nädalat* | week |
| *kuu* | *kuud* | month |
| *kilo* | *kilot* | kilo |
| *euro* | *eurot* | euro |
| *raamat* | *raamatut* | book |
| *koer* | *koera* | dog |
| *õpilane* | *õpilast* | student |

*Leib maksab kaks eurot.* "The bread costs two euros."

*Mitu õpilast klassis on?* "How many students are in the class?"
