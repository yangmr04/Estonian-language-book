# Lesson 26: Terminative, Essive and Genitive-Based Cases

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 166 · § 186–193 · [← Previous](lesson-25.md) · [Next →](lesson-27.md)

## Key ideas

- The terminative *-ni* marks the end point: "as far as", "until".
- The essive *-na* marks a role or state: "as".
- Both are built on the genitive, and adjectives before them stay in the genitive.
- Almost every singular case comes from the genitive.

## The terminative -ni (§ 186–188)

Answers *kelleni?*, *milleni?*, *mis ajani?*

| Nominative | Genitive | Terminative | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| *jõgi* | *jõe* | *jõeni* | as far as the river |
| *sild* | *silla* | *sillani* | as far as the bridge |
| *hommik* | *hommiku* | *hommikuni* | until morning |
| *reede* | *reede* | *reedeni* | until Friday |
| *põlv* | *põlve* | *põlveni* | up to the knee |

*Jõeni on kaks kilomeetrit.* "It's two kilometers to the river." *Vesi ulatub põlveni.* "The water reaches the knee."

*Kuni* can be added for clarity, with no change in meaning: *Ta sõitis kuni Narvani.* "He drove all the way to Narva."

With an elative, it gives a start and an end point:

| Example | English |
|---|---|
| *Tallinnast Tartuni* | from Tallinn to Tartu |
| *esmaspäevast reedeni* | from Monday to Friday |
| *kella üheksast viieni* | from nine o'clock to five |

## The essive -na (§ 189)

Answers *kellena?*, *millena?*

| Nominative | Genitive | Essive | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| *autojuht* | *autojuhi* | *autojuhina* | as a driver |
| *sõber* | *sõbra* | *sõbrana* | as a friend |
| *noor* | *noore* | *noorena* | when young |
| *igav* | *igava* | *igavana* | as boring |

*Ta töötab autojuhina.* "He works as a driver." *Noorena elasin maal.* "When I was young I lived in the country." *Film tundus igavana.* "The film seemed boring." *Tunnen end väsinuna.* "I feel tired."

## Adjectives and pronouns (§ 190–191)

As with *-ga* and *-ta* (Lesson 20), only the noun takes the ending. The adjective stays in the genitive.

| Phrase | English |
|---|---|
| *selle aasta lõpuni* | until the end of this year |
| *noore arstina* | as a young doctor |

Pronouns use the long genitive:

| Long genitive | Terminative | Essive |
|---|---|---|
| *minu* | *minuni* | *minuna* |
| *sinu* | *sinuni* | *sinuna* |
| *tema* | *temani* | *temana* |
| *meie* | *meieni* | *meiena* |
| *teie* | *teieni* | *teiena* |
| *nende* | *nendeni* | *nendena* |

## Cases built on the genitive (§ 192–193)

The genitive singular is the base for all singular cases except the nominative and partitive. It is also the base of the nominative plural.

| Case | *kass* "cat" | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Genitive | *kassi* | of the cat |
| Illative | kassi**sse** | into the cat |
| Inessive | kassi**s** | in the cat |
| Elative | kassi**st** | out of, about the cat |
| Allative | kassi**le** | to the cat |
| Adessive | kassi**l** | on, at the cat |
| Ablative | kassi**lt** | from the cat |
| Comitative | kassi**ga** | with the cat |
| Abessive | kassi**ta** | without the cat |
| Translative | kassi**ks** | becoming a cat |
| Terminative | kassi**ni** | as far as the cat |
| Essive | kassi**na** | as a cat |
| Nominative plural | kassi**d** | cats |

*Mul on kass. Räägin kassist. Annan kassile süüa. Mängin kassiga.* "I have a cat. I'm talking about the cat. I'm feeding the cat. I'm playing with the cat."
