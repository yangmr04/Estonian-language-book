# Lesson 13: Ordinals, Fractions and Time

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 72 · § 67–73 · [← Previous](lesson-12.md) · [Next →](lesson-14.md)

## Key ideas

- From 4 upward, an ordinal is the genitive of the number plus *-s*.
- "First", "second" and "third" are special.
- Fractions add *-ndik* to the genitive of the number.
- The adessive *-l* often says when something happens, including on which date.

## Ordinal numbers (§ 67–69)

| Number | Genitive | Ordinal | Genitive of ordinal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 üks | *ühe* | **esimene** | *esimese* |
| 2 kaks | *kahe* | **teine** | *teise* |
| 3 kolm | *kolme* | **kolmas** | *kolmanda* |
| 4 neli | *nelja* | *neljas* | *neljanda* |
| 5 viis | *viie* | *viies* | *viienda* |
| 6 kuus | *kuue* | *kuues* | *kuuenda* |
| 7 seitse | *seitsme* | *seitsmes* | *seitsmenda* |
| 8 kaheksa | *kaheksa* | *kaheksas* | *kaheksanda* |
| 9 üheksa | *üheksa* | *üheksas* | *üheksanda* |
| 10 kümme | *kümne* | *kümnes* | *kümnenda* |
| 100 sada | *saja* | *sajas* | *sajanda* |
| 1000 tuhat | *tuhande* | *tuhandes* | *tuhandenda* |

In a longer ordinal, the final part takes the ordinal ending and every part before it is in the genitive.

| Figure | Ordinal |
|---|---|
| 11. | *üheteistkümnes* |
| 16. | *kuueteistkümnes* |
| 20. | *kahekümnes* |
| 34. | *kolmekümne neljas* |
| 240. | *kahesaja neljakümnes* |
| 1618. | *tuhande kuuesaja kaheksateistkümnes* |

The ordinals of 11–19 are built on the long form *-teistkümmend*.

Ordinals are written with a full stop after the figure or with a Roman numeral: *2. korrus* or *II korrus* "second floor"; *21. sajand* or *XXI sajand* "21st century".

## Fractions (§ 70)

Add *-ndik* to the genitive of the cardinal number. "A half" is simply *pool*.

| Fraction | Word |
|---|---|
| 1/3 | *(üks) kolmandik* |
| 1/6 | *(üks) kuuendik* |
| 1/8 | *(üks) kaheksandik* |
| 1/1000 | *(üks) tuhandik* |

With a number above one, use the partitive *-ndikku*: *kaks seitsmendikku* "two sevenths", *viis kuuendikku* "five sixths".

## The genitive of ordinals (§ 71)

All ordinals except *esimene* and *teine* have a genitive in *-nda*. The genitive is used before a noun in the genitive, as any modifier would be:

- *kolmanda korruse aken* "the window of the third floor"
- *teise korruse aken* "the window of the second floor"

## The adessive for time (§ 72)

Add *-l* to the genitive to say when something happens. The English preposition varies ("in", "on", "at"), but the Estonian ending does not.

| Word | Genitive | Adessive | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| *lõuna* | *lõuna* | *lõunal* | at noon |
| *sügis* | *sügise* | *sügisel* | in autumn |
| *talv* | *talve* | *talvel* | in winter |
| *esmaspäev* | *esmaspäeva* | *esmaspäeval* | on Monday |
| *reede* | *reede* | *reedel* | on Friday |
| *nädal* | *nädala* | *nädalal* | in the week |

In a two-word time phrase, the first word stays in the genitive: *reede õhtul* "on Friday evening".

## Dates (§ 73)

The whole date changes case according to its role in the sentence.

| Case | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Nominative | stating the date | Täna on kaheteistkümnes mai. "Today is 12 May." |
| Genitive | modifying a noun | kaheteistkümnenda mai õhtu "the evening of 12 May" |
| Adessive | saying when | Ma sõidan ära kaheteistkümnendal mail. "I'm leaving on 12 May." |

In the adessive, both the ordinal and the month take *-l*.

## Useful phrases

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| *Mis kuupäev täna on?* | What is the date today? |
| *Täna on esimene mai.* | Today is the first of May. |
| *Millal su sünnipäev on?* | When is your birthday? |
| *Mis kell on?* | What time is it? |
| *Näeme esmaspäeval!* | See you on Monday! |
| *Head nädalavahetust!* | Have a good weekend! |
