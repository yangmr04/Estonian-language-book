# Lesson 3: Commands, Negation and Adverbial Particles

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 25 · § 14–19 · [← Previous](lesson-02.md) · [Next →](lesson-04.md)

## Key ideas

- The bare present stem is the command to one person.
- Negative commands use *ära*. Negative statements use *ei*.
- The negative verb has no personal ending and is the same for every person.
- Many verbs pair with a particle that never changes.

## Commands to one person (§ 14)

Take the "I" form, remove *-n*, and you have the command. Put *ära* in front to forbid.

| Infinitive | "I" form | Command | Negative command |
|---|---|---|---|
| *ootama* "to wait" | *ootan* | *Oota!* | *Ära oota!* |
| *magama* "to sleep" | *magan* | *Maga!* | *Ära maga!* |
| *sööma* "to eat" | *söön* | *Söö!* | *Ära söö!* |
| *tantsima* "to dance" | *tantsin* | *Tantsi!* | *Ära tantsi!* |

The exception is *minema* "to go": its present is *lähen*, but the command comes from a different stem, **Mine!** and **Ära mine!**

## Negative statements (§ 15–17)

*Ei* comes straight before the bare present stem. One form serves all six persons.

| Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|
| *ma söön* | *ma ei söö* |
| *sa sööd* | *sa ei söö* |
| *ta sööb* | *ta ei söö* |
| *me sööme* | *me ei söö* |
| *te sööte* | *te ei söö* |
| *nad söövad* | *nad ei söö* |

Two extras:

- **Stronger "not"**: add *mitte* after the verb, and keep *ei*: *Ma ei maga mitte!* "I am not sleeping!"
- **Short form of *ei ole***: *pole* means exactly the same and works for every person: *Õhtusöök ei ole valmis.* = *Õhtusöök pole valmis.* "Dinner is not ready."

## Answering "no" with the verb (§ 18)

Use *ei* plus the verb from the question, in place of a bare "no".

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| *Kas sa laulad?* | *Ei laula.* "No, I'm not." |
| *Kas nad töötavad täna?* | *Ei tööta.* "No, they aren't." |

## Verbs with adverbial particles (§ 19)

Like English "come back", these verbs are a pair of words. Only the verb takes endings.

| Pair | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *tagasi tulema* (*tulen tagasi*) | to come back |
| *ära minema* (*lähen ära*) | to go away |
| *välja minema* (*lähen välja*) | to go out |
| *maha istuma* (*istun maha*) | to sit down |
| *kaasa tulema* (*tulen kaasa*) | to come along |
| *aru saama* (*saan aru*) | to understand |

In a sentence the particle usually drifts toward the end, so other words can come between it and the verb: *Ma tulen varsti tagasi.* "I'll be back soon." *Nad lähevad homme ära.* "They are leaving tomorrow."

## Useful phrases

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| *Oota natuke!* | Wait a moment! |
| *Kiirusta!* | Hurry up! |
| *Ära muretse!* | Don't worry! |
| *Ma ei saa aru.* | I don't understand. |
| *Räägi aeglasemalt, palun.* | Please speak more slowly. |
| *Pole viga.* | No problem. / Not bad. |

## Exercises

**A. Give the command and the negative command.**

1. *ootama* (*ootan*)
2. *sööma* (*söön*)
3. *tantsima* (*tantsin*)
4. *minema* (*lähen*)
5. *tulema* (*tulen*)

**B. Make these negative.**

1. *Ma söön.*
2. *Nad tantsivad.*
3. *Me ootame.*
4. *Sa magad.*
5. *Ta on kodus.* (give two ways)

**C. Translate into Estonian.**

1. Don't go away! (one person)
2. We are coming back tomorrow.
3. "Are you singing?" "No, I'm not." (answer with the verb)
4. The doctor is not at home. (*arst* "doctor"; give two ways)
