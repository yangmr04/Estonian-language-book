# Lesson 33: Attributes and Numbers with Nouns

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 236 · § 282–300 · [← Previous](lesson-32.md) · [Next →](lesson-34.md)

## Key ideas

- Adjectives, pronouns and numbers normally agree with their noun in case and number.
- With *-ni*, *-na*, *-ga* and *-ta*, only the noun takes the ending, and the attribute stays in the genitive.
- Some attributes never change.
- After a nominative or partitive number from 2 up, the noun is partitive singular.

## Agreement (§ 282–287)

| Noun ending | Attribute | Example |
|---|---|---|
| most cases | same case | *vanas sillas*, *vanale sillale* |
| *-ni, -na, -ga, -ta* | genitive | *vana sillani*, *vana sillaga* |

| Case | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Nom. | vana sild | vanad sillad |
| Gen. | vana silla | vanade sildade |
| Part. | vana silda | vanu sildu |
| Ill. | vanasse sillasse | vanadesse sildadesse |
| Iness. | vanas sillas | vanades sildades |
| Elat. | vanast sillast | vanadest sildadest |
| All. | vanale sillale | vanadele sildadele |
| Adess. | vanal sillal | vanadel sildadel |
| Abl. | vanalt sillalt | vanadelt sildadelt |
| Transl. | vanaks sillaks | vanadeks sildadeks |
| Term. | vana sillani | vanade sildadeni |
| Ess. | vana sillana | vanade sildadena |
| Com. | vana sillaga | vanade sildadega |
| Abess. | vana sillata | vanade sildadeta |

Pronouns and numbers follow the same rule:

| Form | Example | English |
|---|---|---|
| Same case | *selles poes*, *kolmele õpilasele* | in this shop, to three students |
| Genitive with the last four cases | *selle poega*, *viie minutiga* | with this shop, in five minutes |

## Attributes that do not change (§ 288–292)

| Type | Example | Declined noun |
|---|---|---|
| Genitive attribute | *soome keel* "Finnish" | *soome keele*, *soome keelt*, *soome keeles* |
| Phrase that already has a case | *prillidega õpetaja* "teacher with glasses" | *prillidega õpetajale* |
| *-nud* participle | *väsinud laps* "tired child" | *väsinud lapsele* |
| *-tud* participle | *suletud uks* "closed door" | *suletud uksest* |
| Fixed words | *valmis toit* "ready food" | *valmis toidu* |
| Title before a name | *härra Tamm* | *härra Tammele* |
| First name before a surname | *Mari Tamm* | *Mari Tammega* |

Other fixed attributes include *kogu*, *purjus* and *alasti*. Titles include *proua*, *preili*, *doktor*, *onu* and *tädi*.

## Number + noun: choosing the case (§ 293–296)

| Role | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | nom. number + part. sg. noun | *Neli autot seisab õues.* |
| Total object | nom. number + part. sg. noun | *Ostsin neli pirni.* |
| Total object with *üks* | genitive | *Ostsin ühe pirni.* |
| Partial object | both partitive singular | *Ta otsib kahte võtit.* |
| Other cases | both singular, same case | *kahes toas*, *kolmele õpilasele* |
| Last four cases | genitive number + ending on noun | *viie minutiga*, *kahe euroni* |

## Round numbers for rough amounts (§ 297)

The partitive plural of a round number gives an approximate quantity, and the noun is also partitive plural: *Väljakul oli tuhandeid inimesi.* "There were thousands of people on the square." Similar: *kümneid autosid*, *sadu turiste*.

## Compound numbers (§ 298)

In the nominative and genitive, every part takes the case. The partitive also changes every part. In the remaining cases, the earlier parts stay genitive and only the last part takes the ending.

| Case | 4 532 |
|---|---|
| Nom. | neli tuhat viissada kolmkümmend kaks |
| Gen. | nelja tuhande viiesaja kolmekümne kahe |
| Ill. | nelja tuhande viiesaja kolmekümne kahesse |
| Iness. | nelja tuhande viiesaja kolmekümne kahes |

## Verb agreement (§ 299)

A number + noun subject normally takes a singular verb: *Õues seisab neli autot.* "Four cars are standing outside."

Use a plural verb when the noun has an adjective or when you are thinking of each individual: *Õues seisid neli uut autot.* "Four new cars were standing outside."

The phrase stays singular throughout.

## Exercises

**A. Put the phrase into the case given.**

1. väike linn (inessive)
2. uus maja (comitative)
3. must koer (allative plural)
4. hea sõber (comitative plural)
5. see laps (abessive)
6. noor naine (terminative)

**B. Put the words in brackets into the correct form.**

1. Ostsin ___ (kolm, pilet).
2. Ostsin ___ (üks, pilet).
3. Ta otsib ___ (kaks, võti).
4. Me elame ___ (kaks, tuba). ("in")
5. Tulin ___ (kolm, sõber). ("with")
6. Väljakul oli ___ (tuhat, inimene). ("thousands of")

**C. Translate into Estonian.**

1. I spoke with Mrs. Kask.
