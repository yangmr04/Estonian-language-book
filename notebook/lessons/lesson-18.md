# Lesson 18: Choosing the Locative Case

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 106 · § 109–116 · [← Previous](lesson-17.md) · [Next →](lesson-19.md)

## Key ideas

- Inner cases do not always match English "in", and outer cases do not always match "on".
- Some verbs of staying or placing use a "where to" case in Estonian.
- Finding, buying and reading often use a "where from" case.
- Paths through an opening and things held by a part use *-st* as well.

## When the cases differ from English (§ 109–110)

**Inner cases where English says "to", "at" or "on"**

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| Ma töötan apteegis. | I work at the pharmacy. |
| Ma õpin gümnaasiumis. | I study at the high school. |
| Kohtume restoranis. | Let's meet at the restaurant. |
| Olen lennujaamas. | I'm at the airport. |
| Ma istun rongi. | I'm getting on the train. |

Street addresses take either form: *Elan Pikas tänavas* or *Elan Pikal tänaval*.

**Outer cases where English says "in"**

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| Vaas on aknal. | The vase is in the window. |
| Meie meeskond on teisel kohal. | Our team is in second place. |
| Kõik on omal kohal. | Everything is in its place. |

## "Where to" with verbs of staying and placing (§ 111–112)

Estonian treats these actions as ending up somewhere, so it uses the illative or allative where English often has "in" or "on".

| Verb | Example | English |
|---|---|---|
| *jääma* "to stay" | Laps jääb vanaema juurde. | The child is staying at grandmother's. |
| *jätma* "to leave" | Jäta võti riiulile. | Leave the key on the shelf. |
| *unustama* "to forget" | Ma unustasin koti bussi. | I left my bag on the bus. |
| *mahtuma* "to fit" | Kohver ei mahu autosse. | The suitcase doesn't fit in the car. |
| *kirjutama* "to write" | Kirjuta nimi paberile. | Write your name on the paper. |
| *märkima* "to note" | Märgi see kalendrisse. | Mark it in the calendar. |
| *koputama* "to knock" | Koputa aknale. | Knock on the window. |
| *vajutama* "to press" | Vajuta sellele klahvile. | Press this key. |
| *riputama* "to hang" | Riputa mantel nagisse. | Hang the coat on the peg. |
| *suudlema* "to kiss" | Ta suudleb last põsele. | She kisses the child on the cheek. |
| *haigestuma* "to fall ill" | Ta haigestub kopsupõletikku. | He is coming down with pneumonia. |
| *peituma* "to hide" | Laps peitub ukse taha. | The child hides behind the door. |

## "Where from" with other meanings (§ 113–116)

The elative or ablative is used where English often has "in", "at", "by" or "through".

| Meaning | Example | English |
|---|---|---|
| Finding or buying | Ma ostan turult õunu. | I buy apples at the market. |
| Finding or buying | Otsi prille kotist! | Look for your glasses in the bag! |
| Finding or buying | Ma leian võtme põrandalt. | I find the key on the floor. |
| Source of information | Ma loen seda ajakirjast. | I'm reading this in a magazine. |
| Source of information | Vaatan filmi televiisorist. | I'm watching a film on TV. |
| Source of information | Su häälest on kuulda, et sa oled haige. | I can hear in your voice that you're ill. |
| Way or opening | Ma lähen uksest sisse. | I go in through the door. |
| Way or opening | Auto sõidab väravast sisse. | The car drives in through the gate. |
| Way or opening | Ronime aiast üle. | We climb over the fence. |
| Passing by | Buss möödub koolist. | The bus passes the school. |
| Passing by | Buss sõidab meist mööda. | The bus drives past us. |
| Getting over | Ta saab haigusest üle. | She is getting over the illness. |
| Part held | Hoia koera rihmast. | Hold the dog by the leash. |
| Part held | Ta haarab mind varrukast. | She grabs me by the sleeve. |

## Useful phrases

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| Kuidas ma jaama saan? | How do I get to the station? |
| Kus on lähim bussipeatus? | Where is the nearest bus stop? |
| Üks pilet Tartusse, palun. | One ticket to Tartu, please. |
| Kas see buss läheb kesklinna? | Does this bus go downtown? |
| Ma lähen järgmises peatuses maha. | I'm getting off at the next stop. |
| Kas siin on vaba? | Is this seat free? |

## Exercises

**A. Put the word in brackets into the correct case.**

1. Jäta kott ___ (auto).
2. Ma ostan saia ___ (pood).
3. Kirjuta see ___ (vihik).
4. Hoia mind ___ (käsi).
5. Ma loen seda ___ (raamat).
6. Ta töötab ___ (apteek).

**B. Choose the correct form.**

1. Kohver ei mahu (*autos / autosse*).
2. Ma unustasin koti (*bussis / bussi*).
3. Ma ostan õunu (*turul / turult*).
4. Auto sõidab (*väravas / väravast*) sisse.

**C. Translate into Estonian.**

1. Hang the coat on the wall.
2. She is staying in the car.
3. We go in through the gate.
4. The bus passes the school.
