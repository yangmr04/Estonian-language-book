# Lesson 4: The Nominative Singular and Double Negation

[Index](../README.md) · Book: p. 29 · § 20–23 · [← Previous](lesson-03.md) · [Next →](lesson-05.md)

## Key ideas

- The nominative singular is the dictionary form and has no special ending.
- It names the subject and also the complement after "to be".
- In "It's me", the verb agrees with the pronoun.
- Estonian negatives stack up and stay negative.

## The nominative singular (§ 20–21)

Since the form carries no marker, a nominative word can end in almost any sound.

| Ending in | Nouns | Adjectives |
|---|---|---|
| a vowel | *auto* "car", *kala* "fish", *puu* "tree" | *kiire* "fast", *kerge* "light" |
| a consonant | *kass* "cat", *õun* "apple", *päev* "day" | *kuum* "hot", *külm* "cold" |

It answers three questions:

| Question | Asks for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *kes?* "who?" | a person | *Kes helistab? — Naaber helistab.* "Who is calling? The neighbour is." |
| *mis?* "what?" | a thing | *Mis see on? — See on õun.* "What is this? It's an apple." |
| *milline?* or *missugune?* "what kind?" | a description | *Milline see supp on? — See on kuum.* "What is this soup like? It's hot." |

Two main jobs:

- **Subject**: *Kass magab.* "The cat is sleeping."
- **Complement of *olema***: *Ta on arst.* "She is a doctor." *Supp on kuum.* "The soup is hot."

## "It's me" (§ 22)

Unlike English, the verb matches the pronoun, while *see* "this, it" stays as it is.

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| See **olen** mina. | It's me. |
| See **oled** sina. | It's you. |
| See **on** tema. | It's him/her. |
| See **oleme** meie. | It's us. |
| See **olete** teie. | It's you. (plural or polite) |

## Double negation (§ 23)

Words like "anything" and "ever" do not cancel *ei*. *Mitte* reinforces the negative, and the sentence remains negative.

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| *Ma ei söö midagi.* | I'm not eating anything. |
| *Ma ei söö mitte midagi.* | I'm not eating anything at all. |
| *Ta ei joo mitte kunagi kohvi.* | She never drinks coffee. |
| *Mitte keegi ei laula.* | Nobody is singing. |

The verb still needs *ei*, even when *mitte keegi* "nobody" is the subject.

## Useful phrases

| Estonian | English |
|---|---|
| Väga meeldiv! | Pleased to meet you! |
| Ma olen ameeriklane. | I'm American. |
| Ma olen väsinud. | I'm tired. |
| Ma olen näljane. | I'm hungry. |
| Kõik on korras. | Everything is fine. |
| Mitte midagi. | Nothing at all. |

## Exercises

**A. Fill in *kes*, *mis* or *milline*.**

1. ___ see mees on? — Ta on arst.
2. ___ see on? — See on õun.
3. ___ see supp on? — See on külm.
4. ___ helistab? — Ema helistab.

**B. Give the correct form of *olema*.**

1. See ___ mina.
2. See ___ sina.
3. See ___ meie.
4. See ___ teie.

**C. Translate into Estonian.**

1. We are eating nothing at all.
2. Nobody is calling.
3. He never drinks coffee.
4. The car is fast.
