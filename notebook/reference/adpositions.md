# Adpositions and Case Government

[Index](../README.md) · Reference · Related: [Lesson 9](../lessons/lesson-09.md), [Lesson 17](../lessons/lesson-17.md), [Lesson 40](../lessons/lesson-40.md)

Estonian mostly uses **postpositions**, placed after the noun. Most take the genitive.

## Location: where to, where, where from

These postpositions take the genitive.

| Meaning | *kuhu?* where to | *kus?* where | *kust?* where from |
|---|---|---|---|
| under | *alla* | *all* | *alt* |
| on top of | *peale* | *peal* | *pealt* |
| inside | *sisse* | *sees* | *seest* |
| in front of | *ette* | *ees* | *eest* |
| behind | *taha* | *taga* | *tagant* |
| beside | *kõrvale* | *kõrval* | *kõrvalt* |
| at, near | *juurde* | *juures* | *juurest* |
| between, among | *vahele* | *vahel* | *vahelt* |
| in the middle of | *keskele* | *keskel* | *keskelt* |

Example: *Kass läheb laua alla.* "The cat goes under the table."

## Other words with the genitive

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *jaoks* | for | *sõbra jaoks* "for a friend" |
| *kohta* | about, per | *linna kohta* "about the city" |
| *abil* | with the help of | *sõnaraamatu abil* "using a dictionary" |
| *tõttu* | because of | *vihma tõttu* "because of the rain" |
| *pärast* | because of | *töö pärast* "because of work" |
| *asemel* | instead of | *tee asemel* "instead of tea" |
| *järgi* | according to | *plaani järgi* "according to plan" |
| *üle* | over, across | *üle tee* "across the road" |
| *läbi* | through | *läbi metsa* "through the forest" |

## Words with the partitive

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *enne* | before | *enne tööd* "before work" |
| *pärast* | after | *pärast tööd* "after work" |
| *mööda* | along | *mööda teed* "along the road" |
| *vastu* | against, toward | *vastu seina* "against the wall" |
| *ilma* | without (with abessive) | *ilma rahata* "without money" |
| *kuni* | until (with terminative) | *kuni homseni* "until tomorrow" |

*Pärast* changes meaning by position: *pärast tööd* "after work", *töö pärast* "because of work".

## Verbs and the cases they take

| Case | Verbs | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Partitive | *armastama, kartma, aitama, ootama, otsima, tänama* | *Ma ootan bussi.* "I am waiting for the bus." |
| Allative | *meeldima, helistama, vastama* | *Mulle meeldib tee.* "I like tea." |
| Illative | *armuma, uskuma* | *Ta armus temasse.* "They fell in love with them." |
| Elative | *aru saama, rääkima, mõtlema* | *Ma saan sinust aru.* "I understand you." |
| Ablative | *küsima, saama* | *Küsi õpetajalt!* "Ask the teacher!" |
| Translative | *saama, jääma* | *Ta sai arstiks.* "They became a doctor." |
| Comitative | *kohtuma, abielluma, nõustuma* | *Ma kohtun sõbraga.* "I am meeting a friend." |

## Adjectives and the cases they take

| Expression | Case | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *rahul* "satisfied" | comitative | *Olen tööga rahul.* "I am satisfied with the work." |
| *tänulik* "grateful" | allative | *Olen sulle tänulik.* "I am grateful to you." |
| *huvitatud* "interested" | elative | *Olen muusikast huvitatud.* "I am interested in music." |
| *uhke* "proud" | genitive + *üle* | *Ta on poja üle uhke.* "They are proud of their son." |
