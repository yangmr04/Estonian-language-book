# The 14 Cases

[Index](../README.md) · Reference · Related: [Lesson 7](../lessons/lesson-07.md), [Lesson 14](../lessons/lesson-14.md), [Lesson 16](../lessons/lesson-16.md), [Lesson 30](../lessons/lesson-30.md)

## Overview

| Case | Estonian name | Question | Ending | Main meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | *nimetav* | *kes? mis?* | none | subject, dictionary form | *Kass magab.* "The cat is sleeping." |
| Genitive | *omastav* | *kelle? mille?* | vowel | possession, total object | *kassi toit* "the cat's food" |
| Partitive | *osastav* | *keda? mida?* | *-d, -t,* vowel | partial object, after numbers | *Näen kassi.* "I see a cat." |
| Illative | *sisseütlev* | *kellesse? millesse?* | *-sse* | into | *majasse* "into the house" |
| Inessive | *seesütlev* | *kelles? milles?* | *-s* | in | *majas* "in the house" |
| Elative | *seestütlev* | *kellest? millest?* | *-st* | out of, about | *majast* "out of the house" |
| Allative | *alaleütlev* | *kellele? millele?* | *-le* | onto, to | *lauale* "onto the table" |
| Adessive | *alalütlev* | *kellel? millel?* | *-l* | on, at; owner | *laual* "on the table" |
| Ablative | *alaltütlev* | *kellelt? millelt?* | *-lt* | off, from | *laualt* "off the table" |
| Translative | *saav* | *kelleks? milleks?* | *-ks* | becoming, deadline | *arstiks* "(to become) a doctor" |
| Terminative | *rajav* | *kelleni? milleni?* | *-ni* | until, as far as | *homseni* "until tomorrow" |
| Essive | *olev* | *kellena? millena?* | *-na* | as, in the role of | *arstina* "as a doctor" |
| Abessive | *ilmaütlev* | *kelleta? milleta?* | *-ta* | without | *rahata* "without money" |
| Comitative | *kaasaütlev* | *kellega? millega?* | *-ga* | with, by means of | *sõbraga* "with a friend" |

## How the forms are built

- Learn three forms of each word: nominative, genitive and partitive singular.
- The other 11 singular cases add their ending to the **genitive singular**: *raamatu* gives *raamatusse*, *raamatus*, *raamatust*.
- The other 11 plural cases add their ending to the **genitive plural**: *raamatute* gives *raamatutesse*, *raamatutes*, *raamatutest*.
- The plural marker in the nominative is *-d*: *raamatud*.

## Agreement

An adjective agrees with its noun, except in the last four cases, where only the noun takes the ending and the adjective stays genitive.

| Case | Example | English |
|---|---|---|
| Inessive | *suures majas* | in the big house |
| Translative | *suureks majaks* | (into) a big house |
| Terminative | *suure majani* | as far as the big house |
| Essive | *suure majana* | as a big house |
| Abessive | *suure majata* | without a big house |
| Comitative | *suure majaga* | with the big house |

The translative keeps full agreement. Only the terminative, essive, abessive and comitative leave the adjective in the genitive.
