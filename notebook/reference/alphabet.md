# Alphabet and Pronunciation

[Index](../README.md) · Reference · Related: [Lesson 1](../lessons/lesson-01.md), [Lesson 38](../lessons/lesson-38.md)

Estonian spelling is close to phonetic: each letter nearly always stands for the same sound. English comparisons below are approximate.

Each [IPA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA) symbol links to its Wikipedia article, which usually includes an audio sample. For the full picture, see [Estonian phonology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonian_phonology).

## The alphabet

Full order: *a b c d e f g h i j k l m n o p q r s š z ž t u v w õ ä ö ü x y*

| Group | Letters |
|---|---|
| Native words | *a b d e g h i j k l m n o p r s t u v õ ä ö ü* |
| Mostly loanwords | *f š z ž* |
| Foreign names only | *c q w x y* |

## Vowels

A doubled vowel letter is long, shown in IPA with ː, as in *aa* /ɑː/.

| Letter | IPA | Sound | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| *a* | [ɑ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_back_unrounded_vowel) | a in "father", but short when single | *ema* "mother" |
| *e* | [e](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Close-mid_front_unrounded_vowel) | e in "bed" | *tere* "hello" |
| *i* | [i](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Close_front_unrounded_vowel) | i in "machine", but short when single | *isa* "father" |
| *o* | [o](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Close-mid_back_rounded_vowel) | o in "more", but short when single | *tore* "great" |
| *u* | [u](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Close_back_rounded_vowel) | u in "put" | *uks* "door" |
| *õ* | [ɤ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Close-mid_back_unrounded_vowel) | no English match: say "uh" far back with unrounded lips | *õde* "sister" |
| *ä* | [æ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Near-open_front_unrounded_vowel) | a in "cat" | *ära* "away" |
| *ö* | [ø](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Close-mid_front_rounded_vowel) | German ö; like "e" with rounded lips | *öö* "night" |
| *ü* | [y](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Close_front_rounded_vowel) | German ü, French u; like "ee" with rounded lips | *üks* "one" |

The sound of *õ* varies by speaker, and can be higher or further forward than ɤ.

## Consonants

| Letter | IPA | Sound | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| *b* | [p](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiceless_bilabial_plosive) | short and voiceless, like a soft p | *aabits* "ABC book" |
| *d* | [t](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiceless_dental_and_alveolar_plosives) | short and voiceless, like a soft t | *sõda* "war" |
| *g* | [k](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiceless_velar_plosive) | short and voiceless, like a soft k | *aga* "but" |
| *p* | pː | longer than *b* between vowels, with no puff of air | *õpik* "textbook" |
| *t* | tː | longer than *d* between vowels, with no puff of air | *sõita* "to travel" |
| *k* | kː | longer than *g* between vowels, with no puff of air | *lõikama* "to cut" |
| *f* | [f](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiceless_labiodental_fricative) | f in "fan" | *foto* "photo" |
| *h* | [h](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiceless_glottal_fricative) | always pronounced, even before consonants | *tahke* "solid" |
| *j* | [j](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiced_palatal_approximant) | y in "yes" | *jah* "yes" |
| *l* | [l](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental,_alveolar_and_postalveolar_lateral_approximants) | l in "lip" | *laev* "ship" |
| *m* | [m](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiced_bilabial_nasal) | m in "map" | *maja* "house" |
| *n* | [n](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental,_alveolar_and_postalveolar_nasals) | n in "net" | *nimi* "name" |
| *n* before *k, g* | [ŋ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiced_velar_nasal) | ng in "sing" | *pank* "bank" |
| *r* | [r](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental,_alveolar_and_postalveolar_trills) | trilled | *raha* "money" |
| *s* | [s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiceless_alveolar_fricative) | always s, never z | *sõber* "friend" |
| *š* | [ʃ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiceless_postalveolar_fricative) | sh in "ship" | *šokolaad* "chocolate" |
| *z, ž* | s, ʃ | loanword letters, close to *s* and *š* | *garaaž* "garage" |
| *v* | [v](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voiced_labiodental_fricative) | v in "very" | *vesi* "water" |

Letters *p, t, k* are short [p, t, k] at the start of a word, as in *kala* "fish".

## Stress

Stress almost always falls on the **first syllable**, even in long words: *raamatukogu* "library". Each part of a compound keeps some stress. A few words and many loanwords differ, such as *aitäh* "thank you", stressed on the second syllable.

## Common diphthongs

Pronounce both vowels within one syllable.

| Spelling | IPA | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *ai* | ɑi | *lai* "wide" |
| *au* | ɑu | *auto* "car" |
| *ae* | ɑe | *laev* "ship" |
| *oi* | oi | *poiss* "boy" |
| *ui* | ui | *kui* "when, if" |
| *õi* | ɤi | *kõik* "all" |
| *äe* | æe | *käe* "of the hand" |
| *ei* | ei | *leib* "bread" |

## Three sound lengths

Vowels and consonants have [three lengths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonian_phonology). Length can change meaning, and the third length isn't always shown in spelling. IPA marks long sounds with ː and overlong sounds with ːː.

| Length | Example | IPA | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short | *koli* | koli | "junk" |
| Long | *kooli* | koːli | "of the school" (genitive) |
| Overlong | *kooli* | koːːli | "to school" (short illative) |
| Short | *kabi* | kɑpi | "hoof" |
| Long | *kapi* | kɑpːi | "of the cupboard" |
| Overlong | *kappi* | kɑpːːi | "cupboard" (partitive), "into the cupboard" |

In the second group, spelling changes as the consonant lengthens. In the *kooli* pair, only the voice shows the difference: the overlong vowel is held longer, with a falling tone.

## Softened consonants

Some consonants are [palatalized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palatalization_%28phonetics%29), as if a light "y" touched them, and spelling doesn't mark this. IPA shows it with ʲ. *Palk* "log" is softened, pɑlʲk, while *palk* "salary" is not, pɑlk.
