## Principal forms

Knowing these forms lets you build nearly everything else.

| *-ma* infinitive | *-da* infinitive | Present (ma) | Past (ma) | *-nud* | *-tud* | Impersonal | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *elama* | *elada* | *elan* | *elasin* | *elanud* | *elatud* | *elatakse* | live |
| *õppima* | *õppida* | *õpin* | *õppisin* | *õppinud* | *õpitud* | *õpitakse* | learn |
| *lugema* | *lugeda* | *loen* | *lugesin* | *lugenud* | *loetud* | *loetakse* | read |
| *ostma* | *osta* | *ostan* | *ostsin* | *ostnud* | *ostetud* | *ostetakse* | buy |
| *andma* | *anda* | *annan* | *andsin* | *andnud* | *antud* | *antakse* | give |

## Which form builds what

| Starting form | Builds | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *-ma* stem | past tense, *-v* participle, *-mine* noun, *-ja* noun | *elasin, elav, elamine, elaja* |
| *-da* stem | *-nud*, plural commands, *-gu* commands, *-des* gerund | *elanud, elage, elagu, elades* |
| Present stem | present tense, conditional, singular command | *elan, elaksin, ela!* |
| *-tud* stem | passive forms | *elatud, elati* |

## Full conjugation of *elama*

| Person | Present | Past | Conditional | Perfect | Pluperfect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *ma* | *elan* | *elasin* | *elaksin* | *olen elanud* | *olin elanud* |
| *sa* | *elad* | *elasid* | *elaksid* | *oled elanud* | *olid elanud* |
| *ta* | *elab* | *elas* | *elaks* | *on elanud* | *oli elanud* |
| *me* | *elame* | *elasime* | *elaksime* | *oleme elanud* | *olime elanud* |
| *te* | *elate* | *elasite* | *elaksite* | *olete elanud* | *olite elanud* |
| *nad* | *elavad* | *elasid* | *elaksid* | *on elanud* | *olid elanud* |
| Negative | *ei ela* | *ei elanud* | *ei elaks* | *ei ole elanud* | *ei olnud elanud* |

## Commands

| Person | Positive | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| *sa* | *Ela!* | *Ära ela!* |
| *te* | *Elage!* | *Ärge elage!* |
| *me* | *Elagem!* | *Ärgem elagem!* |
| *ta, nad* | *Elagu!* | *Ärgu elagu!* |

## Other verb forms

| Form | Example | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Quotative | *Ta elavat Tartus.* | reported or doubtful: "He is said to live in Tartu." |
| Impersonal present | *Siin elatakse hästi.* | "People live well here." |
| Impersonal past | *Siin elati hästi.* | "People lived well here." |
| Gerund | *Elades Tartus õppisin keelt.* | "While living in Tartu, I learned the language." |
