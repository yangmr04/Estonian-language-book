# Numbers: Cardinal and Ordinal

[Index](../README.md) · Reference · Related: [Lesson 12](../lessons/lesson-12.md), [Lesson 13](../lessons/lesson-13.md), [Lesson 33](../lessons/lesson-33.md)

Each table gives the nominative, genitive and partitive singular. Cardinal forms follow the Estonian spelling dictionary *Eesti õigekeelsussõnaraamat* (ÕS 2018). Ordinals not listed there are built by the same regular pattern.

Parentheses mark an optional part, so *kaht(e)* means *kaht* or *kahte*. *Ühte* and *kahte* are the emphatic choices.

## Cardinal numbers 0–10

| | Nominative | Genitive | Partitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | *null* | *nulli* | *nulli* |
| 1 | *üks* | *ühe* | *üht(e)* |
| 2 | *kaks* | *kahe* | *kaht(e)* |
| 3 | *kolm* | *kolme* | *kolme* |
| 4 | *neli* | *nelja* | *nelja* |
| 5 | *viis* | *viie* | *viit* |
| 6 | *kuus* | *kuue* | *kuut* |
| 7 | *seitse* | *seitsme* | *seitset* |
| 8 | *kaheksa* | *kaheksa* | *kaheksat* |
| 9 | *üheksa* | *üheksa* | *üheksat* |
| 10 | *kümme* | *kümne* | *kümmet* |

| 14 | *neliteist(kümmend)* | *neljateist(kümne)* | *neljateist(kümmend)* |
| 15 | *viisteist(kümmend)* | *viieteist(kümne)* | *viitteist(kümmend)* |

## Thousands and above

| | Nominative | Genitive | Partitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | *tuhat* | *tuhande* | *tuhandet* |
| 2,000 | *kaks tuhat* | *kahe tuhande* | *kaht(e) tuhandet / tuhat* |
| 1,000,000 | *miljon* | *miljoni* | *miljonit* |
| 1,000,000,000 | *miljard* | *miljardi* | *miljardit* |

As part of a compound number, the partitive of *tuhat* can stay *tuhat*.

## Compound cardinals

Every part of a compound number changes case.

| | Nominative | Genitive | Partitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | *kakskümmend üks* | *kahekümne ühe* | *kaht(e)kümmend üht(e)* |
| 58 | *viiskümmend kaheksa* | *viiekümne kaheksa* | *viitkümmend kaheksat* |
| 345 | *kolmsada nelikümmend viis* | *kolmesaja neljakümne viie* | *kolmesada(t) neljakümmend viit* |

## Compound ordinals

Earlier parts stay in the genitive, and only the last part is an ordinal that changes case.

| | Nominative | Genitive | Partitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21st | *kahekümne esimene* | *kahekümne esimese* | *kahekümne esimest* |
| 32nd | *kolmekümne teine* | *kolmekümne teise* | *kolmekümne teist* |
| 143rd | *saja neljakümne kolmas* | *saja neljakümne kolmanda* | *saja neljakümne kolmandat* |

## With a noun

| Number word | Case of the number | Case of the noun | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| *üks* | nominative | nominative singular | *üks raamat* "one book" |
| Above one | nominative | **partitive singular** | *kolm raamatut* "three books" |
| Any cardinal | genitive | genitive singular | *kolme raamatu* "of three books" |
| Any cardinal | partitive | partitive singular | *Ma loen kolme raamatut.* "I am reading three books." |
| Any ordinal | agrees with the noun | same case as the number | *kolmas maja, kolmanda maja, kolmandat maja* |
