## Telling the time

| Time | Estonian | Literal idea |
|---|---|---|
| What time is it? | *Mis kell on?* | |
| 3:00 | *Kell on kolm.* | |
| 3:15 | *veerand neli* | a quarter of the fourth hour |
| 3:30 | *pool neli* | half of the fourth hour |
| 3:45 | *kolmveerand neli* | three quarters of the fourth hour |
| 12:40 | *kaksteist nelikümmend* | digital style |

Half and quarter times look ahead to the **next** hour, so *pool neli* is 3:30.

| Expression | Estonian |
|---|---|
| at three | *kell kolm* |
| by three | *kella kolmeks* |
| from three to four | *kella kolmest kella neljani* |
| before three | *enne kella kolme* |
| after three | *pärast kella kolme* |

## Dates

Use an ordinal number. For "on" a date, put both words in the adessive.

| English | Estonian |
|---|---|
| What is the date today? | *Mis kuupäev täna on?* |
| Today is the first of May. | *Täna on esimene mai.* |
| on the fifth of May | *viiendal mail* |
| on the twenty-fourth of February | *kahekümne neljandal veebruaril* |
| in 2026 | *2026. aastal* |

Written dates put a period after the day: *24. veebruar*.

## Time words

| English | Estonian | English | Estonian |
|---|---|---|---|
| today | *täna* | now | *praegu* |
| tomorrow | *homme* | second | *sekund* |
| day after tomorrow | *ülehomme* | minute | *minut* |
| yesterday | *eile* | hour | *tund* |
| day before yesterday | *üleeile* | week | *nädal* |
| this week | *sel nädalal* | month | *kuu* |
| last week | *eelmisel nädalal* | year | *aasta* |
| next week | *järgmisel nädalal* | always | *alati* |

For how long something lasts, use the partitive after a number: *kaks tundi* "for two hours", *kolm päeva* "for three days".
