# Consonant Gradation

[Index](../README.md) · Reference · Related: [Lesson 8](../lessons/lesson-08.md), [Lesson 38](../lessons/lesson-38.md)

Many stems have a **strong** and a **weak** form. The sounds in the middle of the word shorten, soften or disappear in the weak form.

## Which forms are strong or weak

| Word type | Usually strong | Usually weak | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nouns | nominative, partitive | genitive and cases built on it | *kott*, *kotti* / *koti* |
| Most verbs | *-ma* infinitive, past tense | present tense, *-da* infinitive may vary | *õppima*, *õppisin* / *õpin* |

## Double consonants shorten

| Change | Strong | Weak | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| *kk* → *k* | *pikk* | *pika* | long |
| *pp* → *p* | *tipp* | *tipu* | peak |
| *tt* → *t* | *kott* | *koti* | bag |
| *ss* → *s* | *poiss* | *poisi* | boy |

## Stops soften

| Change | Strong | Weak | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| *k* → *g* | *pank* | *panga* | bank |
| *rk* → *rg* | *tark* | *targa* | wise |
| *lk* → *lg* | *hulk* | *hulga* | quantity |
| *p* → *b* | *lamp* | *lambi* | lamp |
| *up* → *ub* | *kaup* | *kauba* | goods |
| *t* → *d* | *kant* | *kandi* | edge |
| *lt* → *ld* | *pilt* | *pildi* | picture |

## Sounds disappear or change

| Change | Strong | Weak | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| *d* disappears | *pood* | *poe* | shop |
| | *laud* | *laua* | table |
| *g* disappears | *jõgi* | *jõe* | river |
| | *mägi* | *mäe* | mountain |
| *g* → *j* | *aeg* | *aja* | time |
| *d* → *j* | *sõda* | *sõja* | war |
| *b* disappears, vowel changes | *tuba* | *toa* | room |
| *g* disappears, vowel changes | *lugu* | *loo* | story |
| | *nuga* | *noa* | knife |
| *b* → *v* | *leib* | *leiva* | bread |
| *rb* → *rv* | *kurb* | *kurva* | sad |

## Consonant clusters merge

| Change | Strong | Weak | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| *nd* → *nn* | *lind* | *linnu* | bird |
| | *rand* | *ranna* | beach |
| | *vend* | *venna* | brother |
| *ld* → *ll* | *sild* | *silla* | bridge |
| *rd* → *rr* | *kord* | *korra* | time, order |
| *lg* → *l* | *jalg* | *jala* | foot |

## Verbs

| *-ma* infinitive | *-da* infinitive | Present (ma) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| *õppima* | *õppida* | *õpin* | learn |
| *lugema* | *lugeda* | *loen* | read |
| *tegema* | *teha* | *teen* | do |
| *andma* | *anda* | *annan* | give |
| *kandma* | *kanda* | *kannan* | carry |
| *sõitma* | *sõita* | *sõidan* | travel |
| *leidma* | *leida* | *leian* | find |

## Reverse pattern

In this group the *-ma* infinitive and present tense are strong, while the *-da* infinitive is weak.

| *-ma* infinitive | *-da* infinitive | Present (ma) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| *ootama* | *oodata* | *ootan* | wait |
| *hüppama* | *hüpata* | *hüppan* | jump |
| *lõikama* | *lõigata* | *lõikan* | cut |
| *hakkama* | *hakata* | *hakkan* | begin |

Spelling can also hide gradation. *Linn*, *linna* and *kool*, *kooli* look unchanged, but the sound length differs between the forms.
