# Question Words

[Index](../README.md) · Reference · Related: [Lesson 2](../lessons/lesson-02.md), [Lesson 29](../lessons/lesson-29.md), [The 14 Cases](cases.md)

## Yes-or-no questions

Begin with *kas*, and keep normal word order: *Kas sa tuled?* "Are you coming?"

## People and things

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *kes?* | who? | *Kes see on?* "Who is this?" |
| *mis?* | what? | *Mis see on?* "What is this?" |
| *kelle?* | whose? | *Kelle raamat see on?* "Whose book is this?" |
| *keda?* | whom? | *Keda sa ootad?* "Who are you waiting for?" |
| *mida?* | what? (object) | *Mida sa loed?* "What are you reading?" |

## Place

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *kus?* | where? | *Kus sa elad?* "Where do you live?" |
| *kuhu?* | where to? | *Kuhu sa lähed?* "Where are you going?" |
| *kust?* | where from? | *Kust sa tuled?* "Where are you coming from?" |

## Time and reason

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *millal?* | when? | *Millal rong tuleb?* "When is the train coming?" |
| *miks?* | why? | *Miks sa naerad?* "Why are you laughing?" |
| *kuidas?* | how? | *Kuidas läheb?* "How are things going?" |
| *milleks?* | what for? | *Milleks see on?* "What is this for?" |

## Amount and choice

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *kui palju?* | how much? | *Kui palju see maksab?* "How much does this cost?" |
| *mitu?* | how many? | *Mitu last sul on?* "How many children do you have?" |
| *kui kaua?* | how long? | *Kui kaua sa jääd?* "How long are you staying?" |
| *kui vana?* | how old? | *Kui vana sa oled?* "How old are you?" |
| *mitmes?* | which (in order)? | *Mitmes maja see on?* "Which number house is this?" |
| *milline?* | which, what kind? | *Milline auto on sinu?* "Which car is yours?" |
| *missugune?* | what kind? | *Missugune ilm on?* "What is the weather like?" |
| *kumb?* | which of the two? | *Kumb on parem?* "Which of the two is better?" |
