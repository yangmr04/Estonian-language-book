# Estonian Study Notebook

Private study notes that follow the order of Juhan Tuldava, *Estonian Textbook: Grammar, Exercises, Conversation* (Indiana University, 1994). Use them alongside your copy of the book.

The lesson order, topics and section references follow the book. The explanations, tables, examples and exercises are original to this notebook, so the book's readings, vocabulary lists, exercises and answers are not reproduced here.

## How to use each lesson

- **`§` references** point to the matching sections in the book. Search for `§ 66`, for example, to find where that section is covered.
- **Answers are folded.** Click *Show answers* to open them.
- **Stress marks** are omitted unless a lesson is specifically about them.
- **Page numbers** are the numbers printed in the book. Add 6 to get the PDF page.
